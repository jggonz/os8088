Restoring an IBM 5150 — part three

By Jo · 2 March 2026

The machine arrived with the original 63.5 W supply and a fan that sounded like a hairdryer. “Recap it” is the usual advice — and it is right, but only after you have measured the rails. See the rail measurements from last month, and the reference tables at MinusZeroDegrees.

+5 V

4.98 V, 40 mV ripple

+12 V

11.9 V, 90 mV ripple

−5 V

−5.1 V, quiet

Do not power a 5150 with a dead −5 V rail: the DRAM needs it and you will not enjoy finding out which chips died.

The teardown, step by step

  1. Discharge the primary caps — twice.
  2. Photograph every connector before unplugging it.
  3. Desolder from the component side.
