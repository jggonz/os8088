VIDEO PLAYER - THE ENCODER'S FIRST CLIPS (wave 8)
=================================================

A bootable fixed disk: the os8088 kernel, the Video
Player, and videos made by the new encoder
(tools/os88venc.py) straight from ordinary video
files - scaled, dithered and budgeted for a 4.77 MHz
8088 reading an ST-225.

  VIDENC-HERC-ST11M.VHD  the Hercules clips, for an
                ST-225 on a Seagate ST11M: 615/4/17,
                20 MB - the 5150's own disk
  VIDENC-HERC-ST11R.VHD  the same clips at 615/4/26
                on an ST11R (31 MB), for 86Box
  VIDENC-CGA-ST11R.VHD   the CGA clips (640 wide; a
                VGA plays them too), ST11R
  VIDENC-VGA-286.VHD     the VGA clips, for a 286 on
                IDE: 17 sectors, 15 heads, 250
                cylinders. `make 286-video` is an
                mr286 at 16 MHz, 4 MB, an OTI067 VGA
                and an SB16 with it and NO floppy in
                A:, so it boots the disk. It also
                carries the four benches (VIDBENCH,
                VIDDISK, VIDSND, VIDKERN), which
                save their .TXT results beside
                them - a 286 measured off the disk
                it plays from. VIDDISK streams
                BAPPLE.V88. The whole-track rows
                read into a DMA-safe buffer now: on
                the first 286 run every one of them
                crossed a 64 KB page and failed

The disk has to be read at the geometry it was written
with, exactly as the wave 0-7 field disks.

THE CLIPS
  BAPPLE.V88   Bad Apple!!, 219 s, 4:3
  BCARROT.V88  Bad Carrot, 226 s, 16:9
  TRACK.V88    Trackmania, 12 s of a run, 16:9
  TRACKA4.V88  (Hercules disks) the same with its
               sound as 4-bit ADPCM at 22 kHz, made
               by the encoder's SEARCH - the card
               decodes it
  TRKA11.V88   (Hercules disks) the same ADPCM at
               11 kHz. A DSP 2.x is rated for 12 kHz
               of 4-bit ADPCM at most, which only a
               REAL card can answer. ON 86BOX BOTH
               CLICK every lap of the player's sound
               ring (~1.5 s) and pop at the start:
               86Box's own ADPCM bugs, not the file's
  TRCOMP.V88   (CGA disk) Trackmania in COMPOSITE
               COLOUR, ADPCM sound - on a CGA with a
               composite monitor (86Box: Display ->
               composite) it is colour; on RGB it is
               stripes. Error-diffused through the
               monitor's own model now, the way
               XDC's are: 294 of 360 frames exact
  VGA disk     TRACK at 400 x 225 - it plays IN THE
               WINDOW on a VGA desktop, and the scrub
               bar's thumb follows it. All at 30 fps:
               16 COLOURS, mode 12h - IN THE WINDOW
               too (wave 12):
                 TRK4    320 x 180, 254 KB/s, every
                         frame exact
                 TRK4M   400 x 225, 356 KB/s
               256 colours, full screen, mode 13h:
                 TRK830  320 x 150, 396 KB/s
                 TRK812  detail 1x2, 236, exact
                 TRK8S   160 x 100, 137
               ...and Mode X, 320 x 180:
                 TRKX2   detail 2x1, 292, exact
                 TRKX22  detail 2x2, 158, exact
                 TRKX2F  TRKX2 PAGE-FLIPPED: no
                         tearing, twice the decode,
                         22 of 360 frames cut
                 TRKX22F TRKX22 page-flipped, exact
               The 25 fps and full-detail Mode X cuts
               are on the previous disk

Each is sized to its source's SHAPE on that screen:
on Hercules a 4:3 video is 400 x 193 and a 16:9 one
400 x 145 - both play IN THE WINDOW - and on CGA they
are 640 x 200 and 640 x 150 (full screen). The sound
is 8-bit at 11 kHz. Every clip was made for the
"5150-st225" profile: at most 60,000 bytes a second
off the disk, decoding at most half the machine on
average and 85% in any one frame. What a frame could
not afford is finished in the frames after it.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR
  - no pauses and no sound drop-outs, fullscreen or
    in the window
  - how each reads: Bad Apple is black and white
    already; Trackmania is grey on grey, which is
    the hard case for one bit a pixel
  - smearing after a scene cut: how many frames the
    picture takes to catch up

HOW THEY WERE MADE (the videos never enter the tree)
  os88venc.py BadApple.mp4 BAPPLE.V88 --preset herc
  os88venc.py BadCarrot.mp4 BCARROT.V88 --preset herc
  os88venc.py Trackmania.mp4 TRACK.V88 --preset herc
      --start 3 --end 15
  ...and --preset cga for the CGA disk.
