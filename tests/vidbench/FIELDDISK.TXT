VIDEO PLAYER - THE HARD DISK'S STREAMING RATE
=============================================

One question: how fast does the fixed disk stream a
big file while the player's interrupt takes its share
of every frame? The encoder guesses 60 KB/s for the
ST-225 today, and nobody has measured it. This disk
measures it, and needs no big file copied anywhere:
it WRITES one on the hard disk and reads it back.

Nothing here touches any file but its own:
STREAM.DAT in C:'s root, and its two reports on THIS
floppy. Leave this floppy write-enabled.

HOW
---
1. Boot this round's os8088 system floppy (not the
   kernel installed on the hard disk, which may be
   older than the streaming read), then mount the hard
   disk in the Control Panel so that C: is there.
2. Put this disk in B: and open VIDDISK.O88.
3. Press W. It writes STREAM.DAT, 12.5 MB, in C:'s
   root - it checks the room first and says so if
   there is not enough. The status line counts the
   KB. W is the streaming write, the fast one; A is
   the old append, which finds the end of the file
   again every 32 KB and slows as it goes, for
   comparing the two. If the machine is reset part
   way, press W again: it carries on from where it
   stopped. When it is done its report is saved
   here as VDWRITE.TXT, and its "the writer" line
   says which way it wrote.
4. Press R. About two minutes, and the screen stays
   still for 25 s of it - it is working. The report
   is saved here as VIDDISK.TXT.
5. Press D to delete STREAM.DAT and get the 12.5 MB
   back - or keep it to run R again later.

Bring both .TXT files back (or photograph every page:
Space / PgDn / PgUp page through the results).

WHAT MATTERS IN VIDDISK.TXT
---------------------------
  ceiling, hook 0% .. 75%   KB/s x 10: the stream while
                            a 30 Hz interrupt holds that
                            share of every frame. THE
                            NUMBER THE ENCODER NEEDS.
  data at 12 MB             ok, or the reads are wrong
                            and nothing above counts.
  int13 one track / sector  the controller's own rate.

VIDSND.O88 is here too: the Sound Blaster, and whole
tracks read while its interrupt burns 0-75% of a
frame. Press R; it needs no file. With no card it
says so.
