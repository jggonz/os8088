VIDEO PLAYER - WAVE 0 FIELD DISK
================================

Three benches for the four questions an emulator cannot
answer (docs/plans/VIDEO-PLAN.md section 8). All three
only READ. Nothing here writes to any disk.

Put this disk in B: (or A: once the desktop is up) and
open it. Each bench opens a window: press R to run,
then Space / PgDn / PgUp to page the results. Please
photograph every page.

VIDBENCH.O88  -  Hercules wait states
  Needs VIDBENCH.DAT beside it (it is on this disk).
  R takes the screen for up to a few minutes: garbage
  on the screen is the frames being timed. The results
  come back in the window. Any line saying BAD, or a
  row with no number, is a finding too.

VIDSND.O88  -  the Sound Blaster and the ST-225
  Suspends the drivers, programs the SB by hand and
  finds its IRQ itself. Needs HIBER.DRV on the disk
  the machine booted from. Four rows of 5 seconds each
  (PCM at 30 and 60 fps, then 4-bit ADPCM), then four
  more reading whole tracks off the fixed disk while
  the sound interrupt burns 0/25/50/75% of a frame.
  The buffer is silence, so expect little or nothing
  to hear: the interrupt COUNT on each row is the
  answer. An ADPCM count of 0 means the card did not
  play it.
  The drivers come back on every path.

VIDDISK.O88  -  the ST-225's raw ceiling
  Times int 13h reading whole tracks and single
  sectors off the first fixed disk. Its READ_AT rows
  need a STREAM.DAT of 12.6 MB beside it and say so
  and skip without one; the emulator already answered
  that question.

Run them on the ST-225 machine first. On the PicoMEM 2
machine VIDSND and VIDDISK are the interesting pair.
