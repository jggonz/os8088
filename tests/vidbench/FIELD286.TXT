VIDEO PLAYER - 286 DECODE BENCH
===============================

One question: how fast does THIS 286 decode a video
frame? The encoder's "286-speeddemon" profile (then
"286-vga") was measured on 86Box's 286; this floppy
measures a real one, and
what it reads becomes that machine's own profile.
The frames are the same synthetic set the 86Box 286
and 486 profiles were fitted from, so the three
machines can be set side by side.

Nothing here writes anything but its reports, on
THIS floppy. Leave it write-enabled.

PLEASE NOTE THE MACHINE: CPU and clock, the video
card and its BUS (8 or 16-bit ISA), and whether the
BIOS shadows the video ROM.

HOW
---
1. Boot this round's os8088 system floppy.
2. Put this disk in B: and open it.
3. VIDBENCH.O88, press R. Screen garbage while it
   runs is expected - it is drawing the frames. The
   report is saved here as VIDBENCH.TXT.
   CLOSE THE WINDOW before the next one.
4. Optional, with a Sound Blaster: VIDSND.O88, R
   -> VIDSND.TXT.

Bring the .TXT files back (or photograph every page:
Space / PgDn / PgUp page through the results).

WHAT MATTERS IN VIDBENCH.TXT
----------------------------
  the raw rows    8,000 bytes to the screen and to RAM,
                  read from the screen, the 12h store
                  and the latch copy: the CARD's speed.
  every frame     "nat scr" is the player's own decoder
                  drawing it. OK/BAD per frame: BAD and
                  the timing does not count.

VIDDISK.O88 (X, E, R, W) and VIDEOD.O88 - the player
with the long info card - are here too.
