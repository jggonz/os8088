VIDEO BATTLE ON THE 5150 + PICOMEM 2 - FIELD KIT
================================================

Two questions, one kit:
 1. What does this machine do? The first real
    numbers for the "5150-picomem2" encoder
    profile (it was predicted, never measured).
 2. Why does Video Battle's sound stutter, and
    is the cure ours or the card firmware's?

  VIDSND.O88    a tone through the card while the
                PicoMEM disk is read (T), and the
                card's interrupt rate (R)
  VIDBENCH.O88  how fast THIS CPU decodes frames
  VIDBENCH.DAT  (must sit beside VIDBENCH.O88)
  VIDDISK.O88   how fast THIS disk streams
  VIDEOD.O88    Video Player that saves a play's
                profile as VPPROFn.DAT
  VBATTLED.O88  Video Battle that saves a play's
                profile as VBPROFn.DAT
  README.TXT    this file

COPY THEM ALL INTO ONE EMPTY FOLDER ON THE
PICOMEM'S HARD DISK (C:). VIDDISK and VIDSND
measure the disk they run from. Every bench
saves its report beside itself, numbered: the
first VIDDISK report is VIDDISK1.TXT.

PLEASE NOTE THE MACHINE FIRST
---
 - the PicoMEM firmware (its boot screen, e.g.
   PM2-10-4-26) and its IRQ line
 - the jumpers fitted (IRQ 3/5/7..., DMA)
 - the card's Devices page: SB port and IRQ,
   NE2000 port and IRQ, EMS on or off
 - the video card (CGA?) and the SD card
Close each bench's window before the next.

1. THE TONE - THE ONE THAT MATTERS (75 s)
---
Open VIDSND.O88. Put a phone by the speaker,
START RECORDING, then press T. The desktop
freezes for about 75 s: 2 s of silence, then
a steady 635 Hz tone for 5 s, ten times over:

  A  the tone alone
  B  the CPU held 16 ms at a time, no disk
  C  the disk read, whole tracks
  D  ...the CPU held 4 ms at a time
  E  ...held 8 ms
  F  ...held 16 ms
  G  one sector a read, held 16 ms
  H  as G, the card's line masked for each read
  I  the TIMER held 16 ms, one sector a read
  J  as I, the timer masked for each read

Stop recording when the window is back. It
saves VSTONE1.TXT. Send the RECORDING (any
format) and VSTONE1.TXT. Keep the room quiet;
do not touch the machine while it runs.

What it decides, in the emulator (2026-11-07):
the card's own firmware goes silent in C to I
when it cannot mix while it waits for the PC;
with the fix it does not. On the iron, C to F
choppy = the firmware's; J clean = os8088 can
work round it by itself.

2. THE PROFILE (one boot)
---
a. VIDBENCH.O88, R. Screen garbage while it
   runs is frames being drawn. -> VIDBENCH1.TXT
b. VIDDISK.O88, W: writes STREAM.DAT, 12.5 MB,
   beside itself (minutes) -> VDWRITE1.TXT.
   Then R (about a minute) -> VIDDISK1.TXT.
   Then E if the card's EMS is on -> VDEMS1.TXT.
   SKIP X and L: they are for a 286.
   Then D deletes STREAM.DAT.
c. VIDSND.O88, R (about a minute; it is quiet
   - the count is the answer) -> VIDSND1.TXT

3. A BATTLE'S PROFILE
---
Copy VBATTLED.O88 NEXT TO YOUR BATTLE.VBT and
open it (not the battle's own VBATTLE.O88 - a
double-click on the .VBT opens that, which
saves nothing). Play as you did when it
stuttered: the openings, a few turns. Note
WHEN it stutters (the openings, the menu, an
attack). Close it: it saves VBPROF1.DAT beside
itself. Send it with BATTLE.VBT's date.

4. OPTIONAL: the same with VIDEOD.O88 and any
.V88 on C:, by its Open button -> VPPROF1.DAT,
and whether its sound stuttered.

Bring back every .TXT and .DAT, and the
recording. FTP them off, or copy to a floppy.
