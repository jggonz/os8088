6. Entities

Named: & < > "   © ® — – … « »
Numeric, inside ASCII: A B C !
Numeric, outside anything the cell font has: ☕ € ☃

7. High-bit bytes, declared ISO-8859-1

These are real bytes above 0x7E, not entities: Köln, naïve, Zürich, señor, café, Æsop, ß, ½, µ, ©, £, ×. A fold to ASCII should make every one of them readable rather than dropping the word.
