FONTS/ - the typefaces this machine carries, and where they came from
=====================================================================

Ten families live in FONTS/ on the system disk (SPEC.md 6.4.1). Two of them
were drawn here: CHARTER.F88 by hand, and TALLX.F88 from the kernel's own 8x8
cell. The other eight were FITTED - glyph by glyph, onto an 8-pixel grid - from
open outline typefaces that other people drew, every one of them under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1. That licence asks that its notice travel with anything
derived from the font, which is what this file is.

  TIMES.F88     Times Roman     fitted from Tinos
                Copyright 2026 The Tinos Project Authors
                https://github.com/googlefonts/tinos

  HELV.F88      Helvetica       fitted from Arimo
                Copyright 2026 The Arimo Project Authors
                https://github.com/googlefonts/arimo

  NOTO.F88      Noto Serif      fitted from Noto Serif (Condensed, 700)
                Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors
                https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic

  ARCHIVO.F88   Archivo Narrow  fitted from Archivo Narrow (700)
                Copyright 2019 The Archivo Narrow Project Authors
                https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/ArchivoNarrow

  COURIER.F88   Courier         fitted from Cousine (Bold)
                Copyright 2026 The Cousine Project Authors
                https://github.com/googlefonts/cousine

  JETBRAIN.F88  JetBrains Mono  fitted from JetBrains Mono (700)
                Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors
                https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono

  ROBOMONO.F88  Roboto Mono     fitted from Roboto Mono (800)
                Copyright 2015 The Roboto Mono Project Authors
                https://github.com/googlefonts/robotomono

  INCONSOL.F88  Inconsolata     fitted from Inconsolata (600)
                Copyright 2006 The Inconsolata Project Authors
                https://github.com/googlefonts/Inconsolata

None of these files IS its source font: each is a 1-bit bitmap face of 95
characters, refitted to a cell 8 pixels wide, and the names above are the names
of the faces they stand in for - not of the fonts they were drawn from. The
exact source file, its address and its SHA-256 are recorded in the header of
each faces/*.t88 in the os8088 tree, which is where the art lives.
