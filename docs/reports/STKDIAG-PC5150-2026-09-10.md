# STKDIAG on `vm/pc5150` — the reporting machine's interrupt floor

**Taken 2026-09-10 at `d3c0cb7`.** Arm 1 (`build/stkdiag360.img`, the kernel as
it ships — both mouse ISRs and the ROM's `int 08h` chain already on private
stacks). Hercules 720 on every row, so the adapter term is held fixed
(STACK-SLOTS-PLAN §9.7.2: the adapter sets the mouse ISR's depth, not the mouse).

Three machines, and **the middle column exists to control for the BIOS** — the
fork owner supplied their `BIOS_IBM5150_27OCT82_1501476_U33.BIN`
(md5 `f453eb2d…`, MartyPC's canonical copy), so MartyPC ran the *same ROM
binary* as the 86Box machine. The ROM is never committed
(CONTRIBUTING.md §6); `tools/martypc/roms/` is the gitignored slot
`os88marty.IBM_ROM_PATH` already names.

| arm 1, Hercules 720 | MartyPC, GLaBIOS | MartyPC, **IBM 10/27/82** | 86Box `ibmpc82`, **IBM 10/27/82** |
|---|---|---|---|
| machine | `os8088_5150_herc_gla` | `os8088_5150_herc` | `vm/pc5150` |
| `ROM int08`, own stack | 20 | **17** | **17** |
| **`FLOOR, idle` (slot 1)** | **32** | **32** | **52** |
| deepest slice, quiet | 84 | 86 | 104 |
| deepest slice, +mouse | 84 (+0) | 86 (+0) | 106 (+2) |
| deepest slice, +keys | 88 (+4) | 88 (+2) | 106 (+0) |
| mouse ISR, own stack | — | — | 30 |
| ticks that did NOT chain | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| slice bytes | 384 | 384 | 384 |

`FLOOR, idle` is the row to quote and the panel labels it so — STACK-SLOTS-PLAN §9.8.2's
caveat, which the panel now leads with. The `deepest slice` rows are the
deepest of **all** slices and on every one of these runs that is the panel's
own painter (86Box put it in slot 12 at 106; MartyPC's landed in slot 1, whose
fill reads 288 of 384). It is the answer to *"has anything here gone deeper
than X"* and it is not a floor.

## 1. The ROM is not the difference, and now that is measured rather than assumed

**17 on two different emulators running the same 8,192 bytes.** The owner's
recollection — *"we tested this before and they are almost the same"* — holds:
GLaBIOS is 20 and the IBM ROM is 17, a three-byte spread on a term the
project has previously seen span 18 → 56.

So the ROM term is settled, and **the floor did not move with it**: MartyPC
reads 32 on either BIOS. Whatever makes the reporting machine deeper is the
**machine**, not its BIOS.

## 2. What the 86Box machine costs a slice: +20 bytes

**52 against 32**, same kernel, same arm, same adapter, same ROM. Every task
slice on `vm/pc5150` carries twenty more bytes of interrupt floor than the
same slice on the container's MartyPC — before the program on it runs an
instruction.

That is a real number and it is **not the ~80 bytes docs/FIELD-NOTES.md 42.2.1
is looking for**. Cyclone's worker read **114 of its 192-byte slice** on
MartyPC with both keys held, flat; +20 puts it at ~134 and leaves 58 bytes
free. **The interrupt floor is about a quarter of the missing term and cannot
be the whole of it.** That eliminates the leading candidate rather than
confirming it, which is what the run was for.

## 3. The ladder is monotone, and the iron is still the deepest

| slot 1, Hercules | |
|---|---|
| MartyPC (either ROM) | **32** |
| 86Box `ibmpc82` | **52** |
| **the iron 5150** (STACK-SLOTS-PLAN §9.8.2, arm 2-or-3) | **64** |

Both emulators understate the machine and 86Box understates it less. Note the
iron's row is an **arm 2-or-3** reading: STACK-SLOTS-PLAN §7.5 lists *"the 5150 on arm 3"* as
one of the two readings the arithmetic still wants, and today's arm 1 is what
§9 called arm 3. So 64 is the nearest comparable and not an exact one — the
gap between 52 and the iron could be smaller or larger than twelve.

**What a design should be cut from is 64, not 32.** A margin that looks like
78 free bytes in this container is ~46 on the machine the field runs.

## 4. Three things this run rules out, and one it does not

Ruled out, on the panel's own rows:

- **The ROM chain.** 17, and on its own stack in arm 1, so it is not in the
  floor at all. `ticks that did NOT chain` is 0 on all three, so no sample was
  dropped.
- **The mouse.** `+mouse` adds 2 on the reporting machine and 0 on MartyPC —
  SPEC.md 9.10 working, and the mouse ISR's own stack reads the 30 §9.9.1
  predicts for a 1bpp adapter.
- **The keyboard.** `+keys` adds 0 on the reporting machine. §9.4's finding
  again, on a third machine.

**Not ruled out, and now the leading candidate:** the reporter states that
`SOUND.DRV` **auto-mounts** on this machine (the Sound Blaster 2.0 is real
there and the probe finds it), and that no other driver is. `stkdiag` never
plays a note, so the 52 above includes the driver **resident and idle** and
excludes everything a playing card does. Cyclone plays sound. An IRQ 7
completion landing on Cyclone's slice mid-walk is a frame the panel above
cannot see, the container's MartyPC cannot produce — no machine in
`os8088_machines.toml` pairs Hercules with a Sound Blaster — and that no
reasoning from these numbers can bound.

## 5. How to re-take it

```sh
make stkdiag                       # twelve disks, three arms of four
```

On 86Box: boot `build/stkdiag360.img` in A: on `vm/pc5150`, touch nothing for
30 s, follow the panel's two prompts, photograph at DONE.

On MartyPC there is nothing to photograph — the same values are in §57's
registry as `SD`. `tools/stkdiagread.py` is the QEMU reader; the MartyPC
transport wants the same block read through `os88marty.Marty.read` at
`os88sym.linear("sd_dbg_blk", ("STK_DIAG", …))`, against a **private tree**
(`os88build.tree("STKDIAG=1").apply()`) so `build/` is never a knob kernel and
the symbol reader stays honest.

The box: 4-core Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, MartyPC pinned build, one instance.
