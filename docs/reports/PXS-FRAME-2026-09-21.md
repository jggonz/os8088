## 1. What a wave-2 frame is

Wave 1's frame plus the Textured rung (SPEC.md 97.3): every Textured column
whose six bytes moved (top, bot, the quantised height, mat, side, u >> 3)
has its ceiling and floor put back through the static ladders and its wall
QUEUED — (scaler, SI, DI) into part 2 — and the end of the column loop makes
ONE far call into the part's driver, which near-calls each column's compiled
scaler with `ES:SI` its texture column in part 3 and `DS:DI` its base byte
in the shadow (or the Mode X page). **Still nothing of the sprites, the
weapon, the HUD or the simulation tick (waves 3–4)** — §6 prices what they
will add.

## 4. The fork (SPEC.md 97.1)

**Textured Low res 64 × 80 reads 10.13 (A) / 10.06 (B) on the CGA 5150 and
9.99 / 9.96 on the Hercules 5150, on the full repaint, against ≥ 8.0 / ≥
7.0 — on the frame the gate measures, which is the walls, the ground and
the loop and NOT the quantity the 8.0 line was written against** (that
line was `PXS-FRAME-2026-09-14.md` §3's whole-game row, sprites, posts,
weapon, HUD and sim tick charged). **So the 64 × 80 Low res default is
PROVISIONAL**, and SPEC.md 97.1 writes the trigger down: if wave 3's
measured full repaint at this rung, sprites and sim in, reads under 8.0
fps on scene A (7.0 on B), the default moves to 48 × 80 Low res before
wave 3 closes. The 48 × 80 Low res fallback is measured beside it (13.47 /
13.35; 13.27 / 13.22) and stays on the Size row. Mode X reads 11.94 (10 at
the honest re-price above) against the 6.0 under which the shadow+copy
arm was to be tried: **not tried**.

**What the margin is against, and the model that says so.** The row to
read this against is `tools/pxsframe.py`'s "full repaint, no sim" block,
which prices a Textured column as what the compiled h = 32 scaler HOLDS
(`scaler_terms`: 32 loads, 32 word stores, 16 phases — a run holding an
odd row — and 32 `mov ah, al`, one per parity a run covers: 2,195 clk),
the ground's 48 rows and two ladder entries (1,918), the queue entry and
the driver's call (170), and a residual FITTED from the measured column —
1,916 at Low res, 1,735 at Full. Two rows are the fit and two are the
check: **Textured Low res 48 × 80's compose is predicted 148,790 against
146,702 measured (+1.4%), Hercules Low res 64 × 80's frame 97.0 ms
against 100.1 (−3.1%)**; what remains is the cast, modelled 157,456
against 164,895 measured (−4.5%; −8.5% at 24 rays). The second take of
this section quoted "97.9 ms against 98.7, 0.8% under", which was two
errors of ~550 clk a column cancelling — the texel terms charged a phase
and two `mov ah, al` per run (595 high) and `FLAT_RESID` stood in for a
residual ~470 larger; the first take had called the whole-game row
"pessimistic by 14–21%" against a frame drawing neither sprites nor sim
(the two reviews' finds). Adding back what this frame does not draw, on
the corrected model — the candidate walk 11,000, the sprites' and weapon's
1,092 Low-res texel rows 74,900 at the mean-wall shape (a load, a store, a
`mov ah, al` and the phase on the odd half; a taller post has fewer loads
a row, so this is their ceiling), the posts and transforms 23,900, the HUD
5,000: **~114,900 clk** on the plan's §3 counts, not the 135,000 the second
take charged — **the finished frame at this rung projects to 471,100 +
114,900 = 586,000 clk = 122.8 ms = 8.14 fps before the sim and 639,600 =
134.0 ms = 7.46 fps at E1M1's tick** on scene A (B 7.42; Hercules 7.40),
under the 8.0 line; **the 48 × 80 fallback projects to 10.68 / 9.78** on
the same terms. That projection is what wave 3 works against, and the
room is in the cast's 1,250-a-ray residual and the Textured column's
fitted ~1,900.

## 6. What this does not measure

The sim tick, the sprites and the weapon (wave 3), the HUD (wave 4) — §4
prices their share at ~114,900 clk on the corrected texel model and files
the projection, with the 64 × 80 default provisional on wave 3's number. Mode X on
`_xt_vga` is reported at the machine's own store cost and re-priced at the
CGA-class one (§2.1); what would answer it on iron is 86Box's `386` profile
or the field. Windowed on an 8086 is reported, never promised (PLAN §15).
The launch no longer pays the transpose and the two sets' generation
(SPEC.md 97.1): they are the first Textured frame's — behind the black in
a bracket, the worker's own time in a window — and `tests/pxsbench.py` row
(h) remains their instrument (604 ms + 2 × 130–137 ms on the 5150).
