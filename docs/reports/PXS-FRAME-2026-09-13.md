# PIXELSTEIN 3D — the unit costs, measured, and the frame table re-derived from them

**A measurement, not a description.** Taken 2026-09-13/14 at `5b898f16`
(branch `game/pixelstein`, wave 0 uncommitted on top) on an Apple M1 (8
cores), `nasm` 3.02, MartyPC at the pinned commit `e15cb04f`, guests
`os8088_5150_cga_gla`, `os8088_5150_herc_gla` and `os8088_xt_vga` — a
cycle-exact 4.77 MHz 8088 with a CGA, a Hercules and a VGA. Every figure
comes from `tests/pxsbench.py` reading `tests/pxsbench/pxsbench.asm`'s rows
back out of the package's bss (SPEC.md 97.10): benchlib's PIT-bracketed
method, which MartyPC counts to the cycle, so two runs agree to within 0.1 µs
on every method-P row. It is true of the tree it was taken on and of no
other; a later measurement is a new file.

**This is the fourth take (r5), after three rounds of review.** The first
take was withdrawn on three defects in the instrument (an `es:` prefix on
the framebuffer store rows, a column-setup row carrying the bench's own
scaffolding, a transpose that reloaded the master byte per texel) and one in
the arithmetic (the simulation evaluated once at "1.3 ticks" when it is a
function of the frame — solved as a fixed point since). The second measured
the hit (a bare 630 through two takes; 1,294.5), the dither rotate and its
escape. The third moved the default rung. **This take measures the last D
term on the drawing path — the simulation tick, twice, at E1M1's counts and
at the plan's caps — and the two forms the rotate takes as the 1992 engine
turns it; scales the present and the Δ-fill to the rung (the third take
charged a 48-column view the whole 64-byte band); prices the static ladder
at its per-store figure (the entry had been charged twice); demotes the
XT-VGA column to the correctness instrument `docs/MARTYPC-DEBUG.md` says it
is; and writes the table on the configuration the user asked for — Size
64 × Rows 80 × Resolution: Low res — with Full offered and reported beside
it.**

                  python3 tests/pxsbench.py --machine os8088_5150_herc_gla
                  python3 tests/pxsbench.py --machine os8088_xt_vga
    python3 tools/pxsframe.py            # §2's table, every line of it

`docs/plans/PIXELSTEIN-PLAN.md` §3 priced the frame from DERIVED units and

## 2. The frame table, re-derived and CONVERGED

Counts from the plan's §3, scaled to the rung; units from §1; every line
printed by `tools/pxsframe.py`. **The simulation is not a constant a
frame**: `tk_steps` (apps/tank/tkgame.inc, which 97.8 adopts) returns
ELAPSED TICKS capped at 3, so

    steps a frame = frame / 54.925 ms, capped at 3
    F = N + s × (F / T)   →   F = N / (1 − s / T)        (T = 262,150 clk)

with `N` everything but the simulation, `s` one tick of it and `T` the
tick. `s` is the one term the fixed point GEARS — dF/ds = F / (T − s), 2.1×
at the default rung — and it was DOT DELIRIUM's five-dot 6.81 ms through
three takes of this table; **it is measured now, at E1M1's counts (4.60
ms) and at the plan's caps (16.98 ms), and every row carries both**.
**Which way the hedge runs**: an under-counted `s` makes the FRAME (clk,
ms) a floor and only the fps a ceiling — a frame quoted here can only get
longer if `s` was under-counted, never shorter. (The second take's docstring
had this the wrong way round.) The band the present copies is Size bytes
wide at either resolution and the Δ-fill is Size/64 of the plan's count;
at Low res the Δ-fill writes the same bytes with half as many word stores.

## 3. The XT default rung

**Size 64 × Rows 80 × Resolution: Low res** (SPEC.md 97.1; the user's
direction, the plan's §15 and §16: *"rely on lower resolution full
screen"*). It reads **9.4 / 8.9 on CGA4 and 9.5 / 9.0 on Hercules** with
the rotate and E1M1's tick, against a promise of **≥ 8.0 (A) / ≥ 7.0 (B)**
— 17% and 27% in hand — on a table whose sim term is measured at last and
whose remaining D terms (the candidate walk, the overhead row, the HUD) are
~12% of the non-sim frame. At the plan's caps' tick it reads 7.1 / 6.7: a
level with all 32 actors and 64 doors live is the frame to photograph, not
the one to gate, and the promise is a scene's.

The third take put the default at 48 × 80 Full, on a 64 × 80 Full that
converged to 6.5 with the word load; on this take's units 64 × 80 Full
reads 6.3 with the rotate and 48 × 80 Full 8.2 — the arithmetic did not
move, the axis did. Halving the rays keeps the whole width of the picture
and reads 9.4; narrowing the view to 48 columns keeps every ray and reads
8.2; the user chose the former, and the middle rung of the ladder's "64 →
56 → 48" was passed over for it. Both Full rungs stay on the Size row,
offered and reported.

**The fork**: wave 1's measured Flat Full 64 × 80 frame is FILED against
8.1 / 7.5 as a calibration of this table (it decides nothing on its own
now); wave 2's measured Textured Low res frame at 64 × 80 is what decides —
under 8.0 on scene A the rung moves to 48 × 80 Low res (12.2 / 11.6 here)
before the wave closes.

What the units say about where the D figures were wrong, in order of size:

1. **The hit at 1,294.5 against 630** — 2.1×, and 630 was never a derivation:
   it is below the ~800 its own parts add to (two `MUL14` ~340, the `div`
   163, seven stores ~170, the glue), and the remaining ~500 is the 8088's
   fetch floor over ~120 bytes of straight-line code. 64 × 664 = 42,500 clk
   a frame at Full, 21,000 at Low res. The `px_h[]` reciprocal table and
   the `1/cos` table of the plan's §13 bring it toward ~800, not below.
2. **The column setup at 1,094 against 516** — 2.1×: the fan read and mask
   (~30), the two `px_tan` reads (~50), the quadrant dispatch (~30), four
   `cs:` patch stores (~130), two `mul`s at 133 plus the shuffles (~320),
   the pointer and key arithmetic (~80), and ~650 of instruction fetch over
   ~150 bytes. Wave 1 shapes the driver around 1,094, not 516.
3. **The simulation** — DOT DELIRIUM's five-dot 6.81 ms was 1.5× E1M1's
   measured tick (4.60) and 0.4× the caps' (16.98). It is the term the
   fixed point gears at 2.1:1, so a 2× miss on it would have cost the
   promise at any rung; measuring it is what lets the promise be made with
   the margin stated rather than hoped. The bench's first cut of the row
   read 23.1 ms with 32 sub-tile steps a walk and was withdrawn for the
   tile walk.
4. **The dither phase** — the graft charged one 9-clk rotate on every
   backend; the pixel format wants two on CGA4 (+18.0 M) and three bits on
   the 1bpp backends, which is ONE `ror al, cl` (+15.2 M, cheaper than the
   datasheet's 20 because the EU overlaps the fetch), and none on C160 or
   Mode X. The word-load escape is +4.8 at Full and worthless at Low res
   (§2), so the rotate is taken and the escape is the fallback.
5. **`bt_build` at 604 ms against 300** — 2×, fetch-bound at ~30 bytes of
   code a column pair; a floor, the package's two segments banking on top.
   Launch pays it once plus both resolution sets' generation: ~0.9 s.
6. **`OSAPI_GFX_BLIT1` at 4.8 µs a band byte against 3.9** — windowed on an
   8086 is reported, never promised, and reads 6.0 Full / 8.8 Low res.
7. **The overhead row and the present's +8,000** — two D constants carried
   from the plan: out.
8. **`OSAPI_KEY_DOWN` at 113 µs against 232** — half, in the frame's favour.
9. **The framebuffer store, the texel row, the RAM store, the span copy, the
   C160 expand, the ladder row, the crossing** — within 5% of the plan's D.

## 4. What the bench does not measure

The sprite transform (1,000 D), the candidate walk (11,000 D), the Δ-fill's
bookkeeping, the driver's per-column overhead (240 D) and the HUD are wave
1's and wave 2's to bracket in `tests/pxsperf.py`; they are ~12% of the
non-sim frame at the default, and the cast and the texels — wholly M — are
65%. The sim rows are a SHAPE, measured: the plan's counts with a plausible
body each, written the straightforward way wave 3 would first write it, so
what they bound is a straightforward wave 3 and not an optimised one. The
CGA16 numbers ran on `_cga_gla` in the retimed text mode and are subject to
§88.15's snow on a genuine IBM CGA, which MartyPC's CGA does model but this
bench does not photograph. The transpose is a floor (§1). Mode X and WIN1
on a VGA are priced, not measured, until a timeable VGA machine exists.
