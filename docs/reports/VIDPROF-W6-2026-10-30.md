# W6: the real-mode bracket plays the Victory's files at parity (2026-10-30)

**Machine**: the owner's Packard Bell Victory 286 (4 MB, a Paradise PVGA1A,
a PicoMEM 1.x as the hard disk and the Sound Blaster). **Kernels**: kern_mega
with `MEGADIAG=1` at 9abdcaae (the W6 diag pair,
`make BUILD=build/w6/mega MEGADIAG=1 VPDIAG=time mega`), against kern_big on
the same machine. **Player**: the `VPDIAG=time` build of each kernel's
player. **Files**: Catch My Heart (`09-Catch.V88`, 1,869 frames at 24 fps)
and Sonic 2 - Emerald Hill Zone (`10-Sonic.V88`, 1,020 frames at 30 fps),
both encoded for kern_big on the `286-picomem1` profile. **Profiles**:
`VPPROF1.DAT` / `VPPROF2.DAT` as the owner sent them, read on the host with
`tools/os88vfit.py --profile 286-picomem1 [--reads] FILE.V88 VPPROFn.DAT` at
the commit that writes this report. Nothing below was re-taken in an
emulator.

## What it says

- **Parity, and better on the measure that mattered.** kern_mega's play of
  Catch stalled 5 times against kern_big's 24 and was late 70 times against
  222; Sonic's late count is 79 against 296. The reads run at the same rate
  (503 and 499 KB/s against 490 and 494, within 3%), the frames-behind
  median is 0 on all four, and the whole frame's share is the same to a
  point.
- **Sonic's pauses are the file's, on both kernels.** All of them are the
  stream running dry near frames 690-720 (kern_mega's longest 2 ticks at
  f697, kern_big's at f719): the reader, not the kernel.
- **The bracket reflects almost nothing.** 4 services and 1 option-ROM IRQ
  across Catch's 77 s, 6 and 1 across Sonic's 34 s - against the
  protected-mode bracket's 2,161 IRQs and services at 38.7% of the play
  that §98.3.22.5 measured on the same machine and file.
- **kern_mega has 73K more heap at Play** (524K against 451K): its kernel
  is above 1MB.

## What it retires

The encoder's `--kernel mega` and the `286-picomem1` profile's
`mega_disk = 0.88` (SPEC.md 98.2.3.8): they priced the protected-mode
bracket's reads, where every `int 13h` was a CPU reset, and the real-mode
bracket reads natively. One budget serves both kernels. `xrd` (98.2.3.9)
stays: it is the in-place reader's own cost, on kern_big as on kern_mega.
