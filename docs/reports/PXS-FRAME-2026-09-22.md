# PIXELSTEIN 3D — wave 3's frame: sprites, guards, doors and the weapon, measured

**A measurement, not a description.** Taken 2026-09-22 on branch
`game/pixelstein` (wave 3 on top of `7a88a4a5`), on MartyPC's cycle-exact
5150 machines through `tests/pixelstein.py`, `tests/pxsact.py` and two
breakpoint scripts, the same method as the two reports before it
(`PXS-FRAME-2026-09-14.md`, `-21.md`): a breakpoint on `px_frame_begin`, the
median of twelve consecutive cycle deltas, a FULL REPAINT frame with
`px_force_all` poked at every stop, a TURN frame with the heading stepped by
`PX_TURN`, and — new this wave — a FINISHED frame: the full repaint with the
world's simulation RUNNING (`px_simoff` 0), the player invulnerable
(`px_god`), the weapon drawn and the pose's sprites posted. The promise
(SPEC.md 97.1) is asserted on the full repaint at the default; the fork's
trigger is read off the finished frame.

## 1. What a wave-3 frame is

Wave 2's frame — the cast, the Textured compose, the present — plus: the
sprite pass (candidates from the spotvis marks, the transform, the sort, the
posts through the compiled scalers with the one patch site, `px_touch`), the
weapon (sixteen posts through the 24-row scaler, drawn when the compose wrote
under it), and in the finished frame the world's tick (22 doors, 7 actors,
the keys) at E1M1's counts. Scene C — E1M1's brick room from (28.5, 8.5)
looking south-east, the gold key, the barrel and a guard in view — is the
frame with the plan's scene-A sprite count on a pose the level holds.

**Section 8.2's explanation was wrong, as review r1 said**: the bracket
`px_cast → px_compose` held the sprite gather, and its ~44,000 clk a frame
read as "a ray of the narrower band costs more" (7,251 at 48 against 6,559
at 64). With the gather bracketed on its own the rays cost **5,004 at 64 and
5,429 at 48** — wave 2's figure — and the gather is a fixed **22,634 clk =
4.7 ms** on a frame with nothing in view (27 statics and 7 actors × 9
neighbours probed inline, a static's transposed index precomputed at load,
an actor's once a frame), against ~44,000 = 9.2 ms in section 8. It is
fetch-bound now: ~30 bytes a neighbour probe on an 8088 is ~140 clk
whatever the instructions cost.
