# VIDDISK on the ST-225: what the owner's fixed disk streams

**A measurement, not a description.** Taken 2026-09-27 by the owner with the
360 KB floppy `make viddisk360` builds (VIDDISK's W, then R), built at
`a06a1cea`, on the owner's 5150's **Seagate ST-225 on an ST11M** - the
machine that floppy was made for, and the disk the encoder's `5150-st225`
profile names. It is true of that tree and that disk and is not maintained
against later ones. The two reports are reproduced whole below.

## What it settles

**The profile's 60 KB/s was 40% short.** VIDEO-PLAN 15.8 and the 2026-09-27
profile report both said it was a margin nobody had measured. The silent
player's ceiling - `OSAPI_FILE_READ_SEQ` streaming 32 KB calls for 5 s
inside an `FSXF_RATE` bracket whose 30 Hz hook holds n% of every period,
interrupts on - is:

| hook holds | KB/s |
|---|---|
| 0% | 110.3 |
| 25% | 105.3 |
| 50% | **104.2** |
| 75% | 86.7 |
| 50%, interrupts off | 103.1 |

The encoder's `avg` is 0.50, so the row that sets the budget is 104.2. The
profile takes ~90% of it, **96,000 bytes a second** (`tools/os88venc.py`).
Nearly flat to 50% is the DMA controller doing what VIDEO-PLAN 3.2 said it
would: the transfer proceeds under the interrupt, where MartyPC's CPU-copied
XT-IDE fell 198 -> 99 over the same rows.

**The controller's own rate is 117 KB/s**: a whole 17-sector track through
the ROM's `int 13h` is 72.8 ms - about 4.4 revolutions at 3,600 rpm, so the
drive is formatted at an interleave of about 3:1 - and one sector 20.1 ms,
average rotation plus the call. `READ_SEQ` inside the bracket gets 94% of
the track rate. (At the desktop a 32 KB `READ_SEQ` is 357 ms, 89.6 KB/s -
the gap 86Box's ST11R showed too, VIDEO-86BOX-ST11R 6.)

**`READ_AT` walks 160 ms a MB of offset** (366 ms at 0 MB, 2,289 at 12),
against the 142 MartyPC measured: the walk is CPU, and this is the same
CPU with a slower disk under it. `READ_SEQ` at 12 MB costs what it costs at
0 MB, 357 ms, and its seek to 12 MB is one walk, 2,252 ms.

**Writing is 18.2 KB/s** - 12,800 KB in 32 KB `OSAPI_FILE_APPEND`s took
700 s. That is DISK-CPU-PLAN 6's quadratic append measured on the machine
it was estimated for: the estimate put ~355 s of it in walking the chain.

## What the budget buys, the same clips

`os88venc.py` at its defaults, only `--disk` changed:

| clip | 60,000 | 96,000 |
|---|---|---|
| Trackmania 3-15 s, `--preset c512` | 289 of 363 exact, 240 bytes a frame wrong | **319 exact, 13 wrong** |
| Sonic 2 5-25 s, `--preset cga` | 4 of 601 exact, 4,391 wrong | 6 exact, **3,089 wrong**; still the disk's |

Sonic 2's camera footage stays disk-bound at any figure this drive gives;
Trackmania's cuts are now nearly all the per-frame ceiling's (scene cuts).
