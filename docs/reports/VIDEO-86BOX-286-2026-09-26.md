# Video Player benches on an 86Box 286: VGA, an ST11R and IDE

**A measurement, not a description.** Taken 2026-09-26 by the owner, in 86Box,
on the `286-video` machine (`vm/286-video`: an mr286 at 16 MHz, 4 MB, an
OTI067 VGA, a Sound Blaster 16). It is true of that emulator and those disks
and is not maintained against later trees.

Two runs of the same four benches:

- **ST11R**: off an earlier field disk, `make vidfieldhd`'s Hercules ST11R
  image, because the first cut of the 286's own disk carried no benches.
  So the disk under this run is an ST-238R on a Seagate ST11R, not IDE.
- **IDE**: off `VIDENC-VGA-286.VHD` cut at `18ceb026` (`make videnchd`), on
  the machine's own IDE disk (17 sectors, 15 heads, 250 cylinders).

## What they found

1. **On a 286 the VGA's bus binds, not the CPU.** VIDBENCH's raw stores are
   identical on both runs:

   | 8,000 bytes | 286, VGA | 5150, CGA (86Box, 2026-09-25) |
   |---|---|---|
   | `rep movsw` to the screen | 7.32 ms | 33.81 ms |
   | `rep movsw` to RAM | 1.05 ms | 21.6 ms |

   So the 286 runs ~20x the 5150 on RAM and ~4.6x on the screen: about
   1.1 MB/s of VGA stores. The decoder's worst frame to the screen is **193
   thousandths** of a 30 fps period (the 5150's is 877) and XDC's own code's
   216.

2. **IDE is fast and CPU-copied; the ST11R slow and DMA.** VIDDISK's silent
   player, 32 KB `READ_SEQ` calls in an `FSXF_RATE` bracket whose 30 Hz hook
   holds n% of every period (KB/s):

   | hook holds | ST11R | IDE |
   |---|---|---|
   | 0% | 379.8 | **1,196.7** |
   | 25% | 377.5 | 940.7 |
   | 50% | 319.9 | 627.1 |
   | 75% | 269.2 | 316.5 |
   | 50%, interrupts off | 319.9 | 637.0 |

   The IDE disk gives the stream what the decode leaves it - the shape
   MartyPC's XT-IDE showed on the 8088 - where the ST11R keeps going through
   the decode. A 32 KB `READ_SEQ` at 12 MB is 27 ms on IDE and 82 on the
   ST11R, and `READ_AT` still grows with the offset (it re-walks the chain):
   55 ms at 0 MB to 275 at 12 MB on IDE.

3. **The whole-track rows were a BENCH defect, now fixed.** The ST11R run's
   `int13 one track` failed 40 times of 40 and VIDSND's (e) rows read 0
   tracks: both benches read a 13 KB track into a plain heap claim, which on
   this machine crossed a 64 KB physical page, and the ST11's DMA answers
   that with error 09h. The one-sector row, under the line, worked. They
   claim `OSAPI_MEM_CLAIM_DMA` now, and the IDE run reads **0 errors**: a
   track in 6.9 ms, a sector in 2.7, and 788 tracks in 5 s with the sound
   interrupt burning nothing, 672 at 75%.

4. **The sound and the clock are right.** The SB16 answered on IRQ 5, DSP
   4.05, 150 interrupts in 5 s at 22 kHz / 735 (PCM8) and at 368 (ADPCM4),
   300 at 8 kHz / 134. `FSXF_RATE` handed 150 periods in 91 ticks, 16,483
   periods a 10,000 ticks, as on every machine before.

## What it decided

- **256 colours fit** (VIDEO-PLAN W11). At ~1.1 MB/s of stores, a 15 fps
  VGA8 frame of ~15 KB of changes is ~14 ms of a 67 ms period, which leaves
  an IDE disk ~900 KB/s by row 2's shape.
- **`os88venc`'s `286-vga` profile** is 400 KB/s and 300% / 500% of the wave
  0 model's CGA period (SPEC.md 98.2.3). An ST11R-class disk wants
  `--disk 250000`.
