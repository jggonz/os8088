OS8088 VIDEO DEMOS FOR 86BOX
============================

Three ready-to-run 86Box machines, each with a hard disk holding a whole
os8088 install and the demo videos made for its graphics card.

  viddemo-vga    a 16 MHz 286 (Micronics MR 286 board, 4 MB), an Oak
                 OTI-067 VGA, a Sound Blaster 16 and a 504 MB IDE disk.
                 The VGA videos were made for this machine.
  viddemo-cga    an IBM PC 5150 (4.77 MHz 8088, 10/27/82 BIOS, 640 KB
                 with an AST SixPakPlus), a CGA, a Sound Blaster 2.0 and
                 a 31 MB ST-238R on a Seagate ST11R.
  viddemo-herc   the same IBM PC with a Hercules card.

INSTALLING

Copy the three folders into the folder 86Box keeps its machines in (the
86Box Manager's machine directory, or any folder you start 86Box with -P),
then start the one you want. Each folder is complete: its configuration
(86box.cfg), its CMOS settings (nvr/) and its hard disk (the .VHD file)
sit side by side, and the configuration names the disk by file name only,
so the folder can live anywhere.

You need 86Box 5.0 or later with its ROM set. The VGA machine's CMOS
already describes its disk (user type 47: 1024 cylinders, 16 heads, 63
sectors) and two 1.44 MB floppy drives, so it boots straight to os8088
with no trip into BIOS setup. The IBM PC has no CMOS to set up.

PLAYING

The machines boot from the hard disk with no floppy in A:. The videos are
in C:\MEDIA, with 00-VIDS.TXT describing each one. Double-click a video to
open it, then press Space or click Play. Space pauses, Esc stops, and F
moves a play between the window and the full screen.

The mouse is a serial mouse on COM1: click into the 86Box window to
capture it, and press Ctrl+End (or the middle button) to let it go.
