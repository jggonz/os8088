HERCULES DEMO VIDEOS
====================

Eight videos made for the Video Player on a Hercules card, to show what it does on a 4.77 MHz IBM PC and a hard disk. They are black and white: a Hercules card is monochrome.

Double-click one to open it, then press Space or click Play. Space pauses, Esc stops, and F moves a play between the window and the full screen. A video too big for the window plays full screen.

Sound wants a Sound Blaster, except 02, which is made for the PC speaker (see below).

01-TEXT.V88
  The Matrix: bullet dodge
  80 x 19 text characters
  Full screen
  PCM 11 kHz sound
  24 fps, 0:49, 895 KB

02-PCSPK.V88
  Bad Carrot: an excerpt
  360 x 131 pixels
  In the window
  PC speaker, 8 kHz
  23 fps, 0:56, 1.4 MB

02 is encoded and timed for the PC speaker. The speaker takes most of the machine to play sound at 8 kHz, so the picture is smaller than the others; 03 is the whole song with a Sound Blaster. To hear 02 on the speaker, choose PC Speaker on the Control Panel's Sound page first; with a Sound Blaster chosen it plays through the card instead.
