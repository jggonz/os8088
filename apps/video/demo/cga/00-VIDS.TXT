CGA DEMO VIDEOS
===============

Eleven short videos made for the Video Player on a CGA card, to show what it does on a 4.77 MHz IBM PC and a hard disk: a picture made of coloured text, black and white, CGA's own four colours, and hundreds of colours on a composite monitor.

Double-click one to open it, then press Space or click Play. Space pauses, Esc stops, and F moves a play between the window and the full screen. A video too big for the window, or in colour, plays full screen.

Sound wants a Sound Blaster, except 02 and 03, which are made for the PC speaker (see below). 08, 09 and 10 are for a COMPOSITE monitor; on an RGB one they show as stripes.

01-SONIC.V88
  Sonic 2: Emerald Hill
  80 x 25 text, 16 colours
  Full screen
  PCM 11 kHz sound
  30 fps, 0:34, 1.7 MB

01 is a picture made of the card's own coloured text characters. Text mode is the same on a CGA, an EGA and a VGA, so it plays on all three.

02-BADPC.V88
  Bad Carrot
  360 x 84, black and white
  In the window
  PC speaker, 8 kHz
  23 fps, 3:46, 4.5 MB

03-MIKPC.V88
  Miku Miku
  320 x 200, 4 colours
  Full screen
  PC speaker, 6.5 kHz
  30 fps, 1:01, 2.1 MB

02 and 03 are encoded and timed for the PC speaker. To hear them there, choose PC Speaker on the Control Panel's Sound page first; with a Sound Blaster chosen they play through the card instead.

04-MIKU.V88
  Miku Miku
  320 x 200, 4 colours
  Full screen
  ADPCM 11 kHz sound
  30 fps, 1:01, 2.3 MB

03 and 04 are the same clip in CGA's own four colours - black, cyan, magenta and white - at the card's whole 320 x 200: 03 with its sound for the PC speaker, 04 for a Sound Blaster.

05-STARW.V88
  Star Wars
  640 x 114, black and white
  Full screen
  PCM 11 kHz sound
  30 fps, 0:38, 3.1 MB

06-TAKON.V88
  Take On Me
  640 x 200, black and white
  Full screen
  PCM 11 kHz sound
  24 fps, 0:40, 3.7 MB

07-KPROJ.V88
  K Project
  640 x 150, black and white
  Full screen
  ADPCM 11 kHz sound
  24 fps, 1:11, 5.9 MB

05, 06 and 07 are black and white at the CGA's full 640 width. 07's sound is stored a few frames ahead of its picture, so the card is never left waiting while the disk catches up with a busy frame.

08-A01C.V88
  Trackmania: A01
  160 x 150, 16 composite
  Full screen
  PCM 5.5 kHz sound
  25 fps, 0:25, 2.0 MB

09-A01O.V88
  Trackmania: A01
  80 x 75, 512 composite
  Full screen, OLD CGA
  PCM 5.5 kHz sound
  30 fps, 0:25, 1.8 MB

10-A01N.V88
  Trackmania: A01
  80 x 75, 512 composite
  Full screen, NEW CGA
  PCM 5.5 kHz sound
  30 fps, 0:25, 1.8 MB

08 is the CGA's high-resolution mode on a composite monitor, which shows it as sixteen colours. 09 and 10 are a text-mode trick on a composite monitor, about 450 colours: IBM changed the card's composite output in 1985, so 09 is made for a CGA from before then and 10 for one from after.

11-WILLY.V88
  Steamboat Willie
  320 x 100, black and white
  Live, on the desktop
  ADPCM 5.5 kHz sound
  20 fps, 0:15, 392 KB

11 plays LIVE: on the desktop itself, so the machine stays usable while it plays - open a window, drag it, type. It is loaded into memory whole before it starts.
