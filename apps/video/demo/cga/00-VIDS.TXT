CGA DEMO VIDEOS
===============

Eleven short videos made for the Video Player on a CGA card, to show what it does on a 4.77 MHz IBM PC and a hard disk: black and white, CGA's own four colours, sixteen colours on an RGB monitor, and hundreds on a composite one.

Double-click one to open it, then press Space or click Play. Space pauses, Esc stops, and F moves a play between the window and the full screen. A video too big for the window, or in colour, plays full screen.

Sound wants a Sound Blaster, except 02, which is made for the PC speaker (see below). 08, 09 and 10 are for a COMPOSITE monitor; on an RGB one they show as stripes.

01-COTXT.V88
  Sonic 2: Emerald Hill
  80 x 25 text, 16 colours
  Full screen
  PCM 11 kHz sound
  30 fps, 0:34, 1.5 MB

02-BWPCS.V88
  Bad Carrot
  360 x 84, black and white
  In the window
  PC speaker, 8 kHz
  23 fps, 3:46, 4.5 MB

02 is encoded and timed for the PC speaker. To hear it there, choose PC Speaker on the Control Panel's Sound page first; with a Sound Blaster chosen it plays through the card instead.

07 is the text-mode trick that gives a CGA sixteen colours at 160 x 100. 09 and 10 are the same trick on a composite monitor, about 450 colours: IBM changed the card's composite output in 1985, so 09 is made for a CGA from before then and 10 for one from after.

11-LIVE.V88

  320 x 100, black and white

  ADPCM 5.5 kHz sound
  20 fps, 0:15, 392 KB

11 plays LIVE: on the desktop itself, so the machine stays usable while it plays - open a window, drag it, type. It is loaded into memory whole before it starts.
