VGA DEMO VIDEOS
===============

Thirteen videos made for the Video Player on a VGA card, each to show off something the player can do.

Unlike the CGA and Hercules demo videos, these are not made for the IBM PC. They are encoded to push one real machine as far as it goes: a 16 MHz 286 with 3 MB of XMS memory. On a machine with a slower processor, more bus wait states or a slower hard disk they do not play smoothly. A 16 MHz 286 in 86Box with a 5400 RPM hard disk is faster than that machine and plays them.

12 and 13 are the exception, on purpose, as the 486 in their names says: they are made for a 66 MHz 486DX2 with 7 MB of XMS memory, to show what the player does with a more powerful machine. A 286 cannot keep up with them.

Double-click one to open it, then press Space or click Play. Space pauses, Esc stops, and F moves a play between the window and the full screen. Sound wants a Sound Blaster.

01-BCRRT.V88
  Bad Carrot
  320 x 180, 64 greys
  Full screen, Mode X
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  24 fps, 3:36, 29.5 MB

01 is PAGE FLIPPED: every frame is drawn on a screen page you are not looking at, then shown in one step, so no frame is ever seen half drawn. Its 64 greys are the VGA's own palette, set to greys.

02-T69.V88
  T69 Collapse
  640 x 300, black & white
  Full screen
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  25 fps, 1:14, 30.9 MB

02 is black and white at the VGA's full width, and it carries its SOUND AHEAD: the sound is stored a few frames before its picture, so the card is never left waiting while the disk catches up with a busy frame.

03-BEBOP.V88
  Cowboy Bebop
  640 x 400, 16 colours
  Full screen
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  20 fps, 1:30, 33.1 MB

03 uses PAGE-AHEAD: before a cut to a new scene, the new picture is built up on the hidden page over the frames before it, then shown all at once. It also carries a LAYER: a second stream that only a faster machine with a faster disk reads, to sharpen the base the 286 draws. On the 286 the layer is skipped.

04-MECH2.V88
  Mechwarrior 2
  320 x 200, 256 colours
  Full screen
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  15 fps, 1:17, 20.7 MB

04 is the VGA's 256-colour mode with the colours chosen from the clip. Its frames are allowed to be up to twice the usual size (BIG RECORDS), so a busy frame is sent whole rather than cut, and its sound is stored ahead as 02's is.

05-LASTX.V88
  Last Exile
  160 x 180, 256 colours
  Full screen, Mode X
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  22 fps, 1:32, 35.2 MB

05 has its pixels DOUBLED SIDEWAYS: half the width is stored and each pixel is drawn twice, which halves what a frame has to write. Where a frame still will not fit, parts of it are drawn in COARSE CELLS - blocks of one colour - and sharpened over the next frames. It plays straight out of XMS memory, without copying through lower RAM.

06-SNOW.V88
  Snow Holo
  320 x 149, 256 colours
  Full screen, mode 13h
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  20 fps, 0:15, 8.0 MB

06 has every pixel at the full 320 width, in the VGA's plain 256-colour mode 13h. That mode lays the screen out as one long line, a byte a pixel, so a frame's changes are written in long straight runs; that is what lets a 286 keep up with every pixel here, where 05 above stores half of them. Its sound is stored ahead as 02's is.

07-MIKU.V88
  Miku Miku
  640 x 276, 16 colours
  Full screen
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  30 fps, 1:01, 14.9 MB

07 has a PALETTE A SCENE: each of its 19 scenes has sixteen colours of its own, switched as the scene changes. It is page flipped as 01 is, at the VGA's full width, and each frame is drawn AGAINST ITS OWN PAGE: it is stored as its changes from the picture already on the page it is drawn on, so nothing has to be copied onto that page first.

08-IKARU.V88
  Ikaruga
  176 x 240, 16 colours
  Full screen, 320 x 240
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  25 fps, 0:52, 20.1 MB

08 plays on a NARROW screen: the VGA's sixteen colours at 320 x 240, its rows packed 40 bytes apart in video memory, where the usual layout spaces them 80 apart. A screen page is half the size of the usual one, and half that of the 256-colour Mode X screen at the same size, so video memory holds six pages of it where they hold three. 08 flips between two of them, against its own page as 07 does; its tall picture takes the screen's whole height, with a palette for each of its 4 scenes.

09-CATCH.V88
  Catch Catch
  320 x 200, 16 colours
  Full screen, 320 x 200
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  24 fps, 1:18, 33.7 MB

09 has NOTHING DEGRADED: every frame is the whole picture, in its scene's own sixteen colours, at the full frame rate. A video that does not fit steps down a DEGRADE LADDER in its busy stretches - the picture runs a little behind the sound, a scene takes fewer colours, coarse cells, a small cut, and last a slower frame rate - and 09 takes none of those steps. It is on the narrow screen at 320 x 200, which holds eight pages where the usual layout holds four, page flipped against its own page as 08 is, with a palette for each of its 49 scenes, and its sound is stored ahead as 02's is.

10-WILLY.V88
  Steamboat Willie
  264 x 200, black & white
  Live, on the desktop
  PCM 11 kHz sound
  30 fps, 0:27, 1.5 MB

11-TRACK.V88
  Trackmania: A01
  200 x 112, 16 colours
  Live, on the desktop
  ADPCM 5.5 kHz sound
  20 fps, 0:25, 2.8 MB

10 and 11 play LIVE: on the desktop itself, so the machine stays usable while they play - open a window, drag it, type. Each is loaded into XMS memory whole before it starts, and loops until you stop it. 11 is live in colour, and its sound is ADPCM: it needs a Sound Blaster that plays ADPCM sound, and on one that does not it plays silent.

12-LX486.V88
  Last Exile, for a 486
  320 x 180, 256 colours
  Full screen, Mode X
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  22 fps, 1:32, 70.5 MB

13-GS486.V88
  Gas Gas Gas, for a 486
  640 x 300, 16 colours
  Full screen
  PCM 22 kHz sound
  24 fps, 1:11, 22.0 MB

12 is 05 again, made for the 486: it has every pixel rather than half, bigger frames, and builds each new scene ahead on three screen pages rather than two.

13 has a palette a scene as 07 does, one for each of its 34 scenes, and builds each new scene ahead with page-ahead as 03 does. Its frames are allowed to be half as big again as the usual size, so even its busiest stretches keep the whole picture and never slow down.
