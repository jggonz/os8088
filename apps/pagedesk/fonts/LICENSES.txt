PageDesk document faces and width tables - sources, licences, attribution
=========================================================================

STRIKES (apps/pagedesk/fonts/*.t88)
  Bitmap strikes at 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20 and 24 pixels, regular and bold
  (8, 10 and 12 in PDRES.BIN inside PAGEDESK.O88, the rest in PDFONTX.RES)
  (true italic at 14 and 16, the other italic sizes are synthesised by the renderer),
  rasterised with FreeType (monochrome, TrueType-hinted) from

    Liberation Fonts 2.1.5  (Sans, Serif, Mono; Regular, Bold, Italic, BoldItalic)
    Digitized data copyright (c) 2010 Google Corporation with Reserved Font Arimo,
    Tinos and Cousine.  Copyright (c) 2012 Red Hat, Inc. with Reserved Font Name
    Liberation.
    https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts
    archive pinned in tools/os88pdfont.py (SHA-256 7191c669...f25d0)

  licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-Liberation.txt, AUTHORS-Liberation.txt).
  The strikes are Modified Versions of the Font Software in the OFL's sense - bitmaps,
  some hand-fixed - and are distributed under the same licence.  Following the OFL
  they do not use the Reserved Font Name: inside os8088 they are "PD Sans", "PD Serif"
  and "PD Mono".  Liberation is metric-compatible with Arial/Times New Roman/Courier New
  (Arimo, Tinos and Cousine are the same designs); those are used here as stand-ins for
  Helvetica, Times and Courier.  The strike text files are the SOURCE and are hand-fixable.

WIDTH TABLES (apps/pagedesk/fonts/widths.txt)
  Advance widths (1/1000 em) of Adobe's Core 14 AFM files, Windows-1252 codes 32..255.
  Copyright (c) 1985-1997 Adobe Systems Incorporated.  The notice in AFM-NOTICE.txt
  travels with them.  The AFM files themselves are not committed or shipped.

ART (apps/pagedesk/art/*.art)
  Icons, cursors, the package icon and the .PDF document glyph were drawn by hand for
  os8088 as pixel grids and carry the repository's licence.  No generated artwork.

REGENERATING
  python3 tools/os88pdfont.py fetch     download + verify the pinned TTFs and AFMs
                                        into build/pdfetch/
  python3 tools/os88pdfont.py draw      rewrite apps/pagedesk/fonts/*.t88 and
                                        widths.txt (this OVERWRITES hand fixes)
  python3 tools/os88pdfont.py build     pack PDRES.BIN, the core (what `make` runs)
  python3 tools/os88pdfont.py build --extra   pack PDFONTX.RES, the other sizes
  python3 tools/os88pdfont.py notice    write PDFONTS.TXT, this file and the licences,
                                        which travels with both on every disk
  python3 tools/os88pdfont.py proof     PNG proofs of every strike and the icons
