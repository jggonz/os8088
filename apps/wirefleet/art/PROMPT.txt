Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production pixel-art sprite source atlas for WireFleet, an 8088 naval battleship game.
Primary request: five distinct tiny top-down warships in the style of crisp early Macintosh / 16-color PC pixel art. Five separate horizontal strips, one ship per strip, centered, all bows point RIGHT. From top to bottom: aircraft carrier (long rectangular flat flight deck, tiny aircraft marks), battleship (three large gun turrets), cruiser (two turrets and superstructure), submarine (smooth thin cigar hull and conning tower), destroyer (compact boat, single turret). These correspond to lengths 5, 4, 3, 3, 2 board squares.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF background, no water, no shadows outside silhouettes.
Style/medium: deliberately low-resolution hard pixel clusters, black outline and 4 distinct neutral grays plus white highlights, orthographic top view, symmetrical across the horizontal centerline. No perspective or 3D lighting. Strong clear silhouettes when reduced to 60 to 150 pixels long and 12 to 20 pixels tall.
Composition/framing: square atlas divided into FIVE equal-height horizontal rows. Every ship fits within the middle half of its row height; the carrier occupies 85 percent of atlas width, battleship 68 percent, cruiser and submarine each 51 percent, destroyer 34 percent. Exactly five ships. No text, no grid, no labels, no UI, no watermark. Assets will be cropped and palette-reduced deterministically for VGA, CGA and Hercules.
