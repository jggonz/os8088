Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production pixel-art EFFECTS sprite atlas for WireFleet, an 8088 naval battleship game seen from directly above (orthographic top view of a sea grid).
Primary request: a 4 by 3 grid of equal SQUARE cells, one effect frame per cell, each effect centered in its cell and filling about 70 percent of it, in the style of crisp early-1990s 16-color VGA pixel art (the EGA palette: black, navy, green, teal, dark red, purple, brown, light gray, dark gray, blue, light green, cyan, red, pink, yellow, white).
Row 1, left to right: (1) a small grey cruise missile with a pointed nose and two tail fins, seen from above, pointing RIGHT, with a short yellow-orange exhaust flame behind it; (2) the same missile rotated 45 degrees pointing UP-RIGHT with its flame; (3) a launch muzzle flash: a bright yellow-white starburst with orange edge; (4) a small grey-white smoke puff, round, soft clumped pixels.
Row 2, left to right, an explosion over a ship in four frames: (1) small bright white-yellow flash; (2) large round fireball, yellow core, orange then red rim, irregular flame tongues; (3) fireball breaking up into red/orange flame chunks mixed with black smoke; (4) dark grey/black smoke cloud with a few small orange embers.
Row 3, left to right, a water splash seen from above in four frames: (1) a small white ring of spray; (2) a big burst of white and cyan water spray radiating outward like a crown, bright white center; (3) a wider broken ring of cyan and white droplets; (4) two thin fading concentric cyan ripple rings.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF background in every cell, no water drawn, no grid lines, no cell borders, no shadows outside the effect.
Style/medium: deliberately low-resolution hard pixel clusters, hard edges, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no blur, limited palette. Strong clear silhouettes that still read when each cell is reduced to 24 by 24 pixels.
Composition/framing: exactly twelve effects, 4 columns by 3 rows, equal spacing, nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell. No text, no labels, no numbers, no UI, no watermark.
