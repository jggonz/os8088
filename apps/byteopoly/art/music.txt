# Byteopoly's songs (SPEC.md 131.9.5, 133.8). ORIGINAL music, written for
# this game: no melody here is taken from any other game or song. The source
# of the song bank BP_A2_MUSIC; tools/os88gkmus.py compiles it, and
# `tools/os88gkmus.py render apps/byteopoly/art/music.txt --song title --wav
# x.wav` plays it on the host. The ORDER OF THE SONGS is the song number
# bp_app.c's BP_MUS_* name (tests/unit/t_gkmus.py holds the two together).
# Voice 1 is the melody: it is all the PC speaker plays.
#
# The tick is the OS's 18.2 Hz, so `beat` (ticks a quarter note) sets the
# tempo: 8 = 136.5 BPM, 12 = 91 BPM, 6 = 182 BPM.

# ---- instruments: OPL2 registers, modulator then carrier, then C0h --------
# Carrier levels (40h) sit 9..15 dB down: the effects play on the tone
# tier's channel 8 at full level, over the music.
patch chip          # the title's lead: a bright, held square-ish voice
  mod 20=21 40=1A 60=F2 80=24 E0=00
  car 20=21 40=12 60=F2 80=44 E0=00
  c0  0C
end
patch pluck         # bass: a plucked, decaying low voice
  mod 20=01 40=1E 60=F5 80=56 E0=00
  car 20=01 40=0E 60=F3 80=55 E0=00
  c0  06
end
patch keys          # chord stabs
  mod 20=02 40=24 60=F6 80=56 E0=00
  car 20=01 40=16 60=F5 80=56 E0=00
  c0  04
end
patch mallet        # the in-game lead: soft, round, decaying
  mod 20=01 40=2A 60=F5 80=75 E0=00
  car 20=01 40=12 60=F4 80=74 E0=00
  c0  02
end
patch pad           # in-game harmony: a slow, quiet swell
  mod 20=21 40=2C 60=74 80=26 E0=00
  car 20=21 40=1A 60=73 80=26 E0=01
  c0  00
end
patch tick          # the auction's clock
  mod 20=04 40=22 60=F8 80=F8 E0=00
  car 20=01 40=16 60=F8 80=F8 E0=00
  c0  00
end
patch brass         # the fanfare
  mod 20=21 40=16 60=B2 80=16 E0=00
  car 20=21 40=0E 60=92 80=16 E0=00
  c0  0E
end
