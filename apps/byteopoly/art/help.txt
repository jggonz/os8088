# Byteopoly's built-in rules and key list (SPEC.md 131.2, 131.10): one line a
# row, plain ASCII, 47 characters at most. tools/os88bpart.py packs it into
# BPART.BIN as BP_A_HELP; the Rules and Keys panels page it.
RULES 1/4  The goal
2-4 players start on BOOT with 1,500 cr.
Roll two dice, move clockwise, land.
Buy the computer, or it goes to auction.
Pass or land on BOOT: collect 200 cr.
Rent goes to the owner of the space.
Last player standing wins; broke players
go bankrupt and leave the match.
RULES 2/4  Collections and upgrades
Own every member of a collection: base
rent doubles while none is mortgaged.
Then add RAM chips, 1 to 4 a computer,
and a Workshop as the fifth step.
Build and sell evenly across the set.
Selling an upgrade returns half its cost.
Rent at levels 1-5: 5 15 30 45 60 x base.
POWER and BBS: 4 x dice, 10 x with both.
RULES 3/4  Corners, cards, jail
REPAIR SHOP: visiting is free. CRASH! sends
you there with no salary. Pay 50 cr or use
a Recovery Disk, or roll doubles to leave;
the third failed try costs 50 cr.
Floppy Find and System Error draw a card.
Import Duty costs 100 cr, E-waste Fee 75.
Three doubles in a row also land you there.
RULES 4/4  Auctions, trades, debt
A declined buy opens an auction: bids are
multiples of 10 cr, and you may pass.
Trade properties, cash and disks one to
one; the other player accepts or refuses.
A mortgage pays half price; redeeming
costs the loan plus 10 percent.
Short of cash? Sell upgrades, mortgage,
settle the debt or declare bankruptcy.
KEYS 1/2  The board
Space    Roll the dice
B        Buy the computer you landed on
A        Start an auction instead
T        Propose a trade
C        Show your collection
M        Manage: upgrade, sell, mortgage
E        End your turn
KEYS 2/2  Everywhere
F2       Save the match
F3       Load a saved match
Esc      Back, close a panel, open the menu
Tab      Move the focus
Enter    Activate the focused item
Arrows   Move between spaces and buttons
Mouse    Click a space or a button
