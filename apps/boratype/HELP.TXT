BORAT TEACHES TYPING

Choose one of four profiles (File > Profile, or N at the menu). Type a name
up to twelve characters, then Enter. Continue recommends your first lesson
that has not reached mastery. All twenty-four lessons remain selectable.

REST YOUR FINGERS
Put your left index on F and right index on J; find their physical ridges.
Left hand: A S D F, right hand: J K L ;. Relax, look at the screen, and use
the opposite hand's Shift for a capital or shifted punctuation mark.

TYPING RULES
Type the highlighted character exactly, including case, spaces and marks.
A correct printable character advances. A wrong printable character adds
an error and leaves the target in place. Backspace retracts accepted text
in the current passage, resets your streak, and keeps historical attempts.
Modifier-only, function and navigation keys do not count as mistakes.

ACCURACY AND SPEED
Accuracy is correct printable attempts divided by all printable attempts,
rounded to the nearest percent. Master a whole lesson at 95% or better;
speed never blocks learning. WPM counts net accepted characters, including
spaces and punctuation, at five characters per word. Retyping a retracted
character earns no extra net speed credit. A dash means no timed sample.
Lesson timing begins on the first printable attempt, including a wrong one.

CHALLENGES
Choose one, three or five minutes and home row, letters, digits or mixed.
The ready countdown is outside scored time. A test ends on its deadline
even if you stop typing. Pause, focus loss and display transitions make a
challenge practice/unranked; it can continue but cannot replace a record.
Records compare the same duration and key group.

PRACTICE
Practice groups repeat valid drills. Recommended weak keys need at least
twenty samples; a result is not called weak from one unfortunate press.
Practice These Keys on results selects the weak-key drill.

KEYBOARD HINTS
Finger zones show the recommended hand/finger. An outline marks the target,
an error symbol marks the attempted wrong key, and shifted symbols also
mark Shift. The letter I remains on the top row; J is the home anchor.
Clicking a pictured key gives a hint and never adds scored input.

CONTROLS
F1 Help   F2 Lessons   F3 Master sound   F4 Pause/Resume   F6 Full screen
Ctrl+Q closes the application, including either fullscreen path.
Escape opens/resumes Pause while typing. Help, Settings and Records return
to the paused lesson when opened during a run; elsewhere it returns to Menu.
Menu letters: L Lessons, P Practice, C Challenge, R Records, S Settings,
N Profile. Ordinary letters and Space are typing input during a lesson.
Up/Down chooses a lesson or challenge key group; Enter starts or continues.
Practice: 1-4 chooses a group, W chooses weak keys. Settings: M Music,
E Effects, K key ticks, R roast, D display, T test music.
Native menu actions provide equivalents for the function keys.

WINDOW AND DISPLAY
The layout follows the real window size and adapter. The normal native
full-screen view uses the OS fullscreen latch. On CGA, Settings can select
the separate 320x200 four-color fullscreen path; F6 always returns from it.
The 640x200 monochrome native window/fullscreen path remains available.
Set EGA/VGA/VESA desktop modes in the OS Control Panel; only the installed
driver can offer 800x600 or 1024x768. Unsupported choices are not simulated.
Focus loss pauses the session; return and intentionally Resume.

SOUND AND BORAT
Music, effects and optional key ticks have separate settings.
The PC speaker plays the melody and short effects. Supported FM hardware
adds three arranged voices through the OS sound driver. The OS chooses the
output route. Music does not require a digital voice pack.
Gentle, Classic (default) and Extra Roast choose reaction intensity. Jokes
respond to typing events and do not change the score or consume your input.
Captions are original jokes. Reactions use visual expressions and short
speaker/FM effects. The application uses no recorded voice clips.

SAVING
Progress and settings save at session, profile and settings boundaries in
SYSTEM/APPDATA on the application's own disk. BORATA.BTT and BORATB.BTT
alternate validated generations. Keep the disk writable and leave space.
If a write fails, play continues and the previous valid copy is retained.
A second instance is read-only for shared progress; close it and reopen
after the writer exits to become a writer. It cannot overwrite stale data.
