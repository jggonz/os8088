## Using this disk

RealDMG, the Game Boy emulator, at the top of one floppy. Put it in drive B,
double-click **B:**, then **REALDMG.O88**. The project's own demo cartridge,
`GBDEMO.GB`, is in `MEDIA`: double-click it to start RealDMG with it. RealDMG
keeps its settings in `SYSTEM/APPDATA` on this disk, so leave it writable.

The other emulators, the Commodore 64, the Apple II Plus and CP/M, each have a
disk of their own.
