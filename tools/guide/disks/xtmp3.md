## Using this disk

XTMP3, the MP3 player, with three demo songs, on a floppy of its own. Start
os8088 from the system disk of the same size in drive A, then put this disk
in drive B and double-click **B:**. Double-click **XTMP3.O88**, or
double-click a song in `MEDIA` to open XTMP3 and play it. See
[[XTMP3.O88]].

## What is on it

- **XTMP3.O88**: the player.
- **MEDIA**: three demo songs. [[NEONDRV.MP3]] is CD-rate stereo,
  [[NIGHTSIG.MP3]] half that rate, and [[AFTERHRS.MP3]] 11 kHz mono, the
  song an XT is meant for.
- **SYSTEM/APPDATA**: where XTMP3 keeps its list of decoded songs. Leave the
  disk writable.

Every size carries the same files. A computer that cannot play a song as it
decodes it decodes it to a hard disk first, so a PC or XT also needs a hard
disk.
