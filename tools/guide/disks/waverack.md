## Using this disk

WaveRack, the sound recorder and WAV editor, with clips of real music, on a
floppy of its own. Start os8088 from the system disk of the same size in
drive A, then put this disk in drive B and double-click **B:**. Double-click
**WAVERACK.O88**, then open a clip with **File > Open...**. See
[[WAVERACK.O88]].

## What is on it

- **WAVERACK.O88**: the recorder and editor.
- **MEDIA**: short excerpts of public-domain and CC0 recordings: Für Elise,
  the Maple Leaf Rag played by the US Marine Band in 1906, Satie's first
  Gymnopédie, The Stars and Stripes Forever, a Chopin nocturne and a Bach
  prelude. Most are 8-bit mono at 11,025 Hz; the nocturne is 22,050 Hz and
  the prelude 16-bit stereo, to show how WaveRack plays other formats.
- **CREDITS.TXT**: where each recording comes from, its performer and its
  licence.
- **CLIPS.TXT**: which clips this size of disk carries.
- **SYSTEM/APPDATA**: where WaveRack keeps note of a take while it records,
  so a take interrupted by a crash can be recovered. Leave the disk writable.

The 1.44MB and 1.2MB disks carry all six clips. The 720KB disk carries four
and the 360KB disk two, leaving room to record a short take: on a floppy
WaveRack records at 8,000 Hz. Playing and recording need a Sound Blaster.
