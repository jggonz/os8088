## Using this disk

MIDIRack, the MIDI player, with songs and an instrument bank, on a floppy of
its own. Start os8088 from the system disk of the same size in drive A,
then put this disk in drive B and double-click **B:**. Double-click
**MIDIRACK.O88**, or double-click a song. See [[MIDIRACK.O88]].

## What is on it

- **MIDIRACK.O88**: the player. Opened on its own, it finds the songs on
  this disk and loads the first one.
- **MIDIRACK.BNK**: real instrument samples for the **SB wavetable (bank)**
  sound, played on a Sound Blaster.
- **MEDIA/MIDI**: ten songs, from Für Elise and the Can-Can to Korobeiniki.
- **SYSTEM/APPDATA**: where MIDIRack keeps its settings. Leave the disk
  writable.

MIDIRack needs no sound card: with none it plays through the PC speaker. It
also plays through an AdLib, a Sound Blaster, a Covox or a MIDI module on an
MPU-401 interface.

Every size carries the same files.
