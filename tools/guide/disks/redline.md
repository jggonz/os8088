## Using this disk

REDLINE, the benchmark that measures how fast your PC is, on a floppy of its
own. Start os8088 from the system disk of the same size in drive A, then put
this disk in drive B and double-click **B:**. Double-click **REDLINE.O88**
to start it. See [[REDLINE.O88]].

## What is on it

- **REDLINE.O88**: the benchmark. It times its workloads against the
  original 4.77 MHz IBM PC and lists the processor, memory, video adapter
  and BIOS it finds.
- **README.TXT**: notes on the program.

REDLINE needs no special hardware. On a 286 or 386, its **Tune** tab can
also make the expansion bus faster for 8-bit cards.

Every size carries the same files.
