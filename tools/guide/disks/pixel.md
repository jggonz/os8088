## Using this disk

PiXEL, the picture viewer and editor, with sample pictures, on a floppy of its
own. Start os8088 from the system disk of the same size in drive A, then put
this disk in drive B and double-click **B:**. Double-click **PIXEL.O88** to
start it, or double-click a `.JPG`, `.PNG` or `.PCX` picture in `PICTURES`
to open it straight away. See [[PIXEL.O88]].

## What is on it

- **PIXEL.O88**: the program.
- **PICTURES**: sample photos and drawings in JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP and PCX.
- **SYSTEM/APPDATA**: where PiXEL keeps its settings. Leave the disk
  writable.

## What each size carries

| Size | Pictures |
|---|---|
| 1.44MB and 1.2MB | All ten |
| 720KB | Nine: HOUSE.PNG is left out |
| 360KB | Five: VACATION, LAKE, CAT, BALLOONS and BOUNCE |

PiXEL works on VGA, EGA, CGA and Hercules, and wants plenty of free memory
for a large photo.
