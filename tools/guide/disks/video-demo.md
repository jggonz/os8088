## Using this drive

A hard disk with os8088 already installed and a set of demo videos in
`MEDIA`, made for a 4.77 MHz IBM PC with a hard disk. Each drive is made for
one display card; `00-VIDS.TXT` in `MEDIA` lists the videos and what each
one needs.

Open a video by double-clicking it, then press Space or click Play. Esc
stops, and F switches between the window and the full screen. Sound wants a
Sound Blaster, apart from the one video made for the PC speaker.
