## Using this drive

A hard disk with os8088 already installed and a set of demo videos in
`MEDIA`. Each drive is made for one display card; `00-VIDS.TXT` in `MEDIA`
lists the videos, what each one shows off and what it needs.

The CGA and Hercules drives are 31 MB disks for a 4.77 MHz IBM PC or XT with
a Seagate ST11R controller. The VGA drive is a 504 MB IDE disk, and its videos
were made for a 16 MHz 286 with 3 MB of XMS memory - two of them, marked 486,
for a 66 MHz 486DX2 with 7 MB. A slower machine plays them, but not smoothly.

Open a video by double-clicking it, then press Space or click Play. Esc
stops, and F switches between the window and the full screen. Sound wants a
Sound Blaster, apart from the videos made for the PC speaker.
