## Using this disk

An Apple II Plus in a window, on a floppy of its own. Start os8088 from the
system disk of the same size in drive A, then put this disk in drive B and
double-click **B:**. Open the `APPLE2` folder and double-click
**APPLE2.O88**. See [[APPLE2.O88]].

## What is on it

The `APPLE2` folder holds everything:

- **APPLE2.O88**: the Apple II Plus, with Applesoft BASIC, the monitor and
  the character set inside the file.
- **WELCOME.BAS**: a short Applesoft demonstration. Double-click it and the
  Apple starts with it loaded; type `RUN` at the `]` prompt.
- **README.TXT**: notes about the program.
- **COPYING**: its licence.

The Apple needs a 49KB block of free memory. It runs very slowly on a 4.77
MHz PC or XT, is usable on a 386 and comfortable on a 486.

Every size carries the same files.
