## Using this disk

Lemmings, the 1991 game, on a floppy of its own. Start os8088 from the
system disk of the same size in drive A, then put this disk in drive B and
double-click **B:**. Open the **LEMMINGS** folder and double-click
**LEMMINGS.O88** to start it. See [[LEMMINGS.O88]].

## What is on it

- **LEMMINGS.O88**: the game.
- **README.TXT**: the keys, what is on the disk, and where the game came from.
- **LEVELS.TXT**: which levels this size of disk carries, and why the rest
  are not on it.
- **LEM\*.LEM**: the game's data, converted from the original's files. Every
  one of them must stay in the folder with the program.

The 1.44MB and 1.2MB disks carry all 120 levels, the 720KB disk 116, and
the 360KB disk 53: two of the five graphic sets and the levels drawn with
them. The rest are still in the list, in grey, with the reason.
