## Using this disk

The music disk goes in drive B in place of the software disk, when you want
music to play. It carries two players of its own at its root: MIDIRack
([[MIDIRACK.O88]]), with its ten songs in `MEDIA/MIDI`, and XTMP3
([[XTMP3.O88]]), with [[AFTERHRS.MP3]] in `MEDIA`, the MP3 an XT is meant
for. Double-click a song to open its player.

At 360KB the tracker module does not fit beside the programs, so it rides
here too, in `MEDIA`. Start Tracker from the software disk, then swap disks
and open the module from this one. The larger software disks carry the
module and MIDIRack themselves, and the 1.44MB one carries XTMP3.
