# Intro fanfare

**Title Fanfare**, a short piece of music written for os8088 in the style of
a game's title screen. A brass lead and a French horn carry the tune over
strings, a tuba, timpani and a marching snare, and it ends on a timpani roll
and a last loud chord. It plays for about 33 seconds at 110 beats a minute.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. The fanfare opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

It is an original composition, made for os8088 and quoting no existing
tune. The music and this file are released into the public domain under
CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music
disk, MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live
USB image and CD. The 720KB software disk has room for only two songs, and
this is one of them.

## Playing it

Double-click **INTRO.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it from
MIDIRack's playlist. It plays on an FM chip, a Sound Blaster or the PC
speaker, whichever the machine has.
