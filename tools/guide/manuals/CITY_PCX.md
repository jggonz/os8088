# City

A city skyline at dusk seen from above: skyscrapers lit against a deep
blue and orange sky, with bridges and a river below. It is a 320 by 240
PCX picture in 256 colours, the format of PC Paintbrush and many DOS
programs.

![City open in PiXEL.](shot:city-pcx)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB sizes. The 360KB PiXEL disk leaves it out for room.

## Opening it

Double-click **CITY.PCX** and PiXEL ([[PIXEL.O88]]) opens with the picture at its own size.
