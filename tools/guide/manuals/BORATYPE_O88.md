# Borat Teaches Typing

Borat Teaches Typing is a touch-typing course with Borat as your coach. It has
24 lessons that build up from the home row to numbers and symbols, practice
drills for the keys you miss most, and timed challenges of one, three or five
minutes. Four people can each keep their own profile, records and
achievements. Borat reacts to how you type with a face and a line of his own,
and the jokes never change your score.

## Starting Borat Teaches Typing

The program is `BORATYPE.O88`. On its own floppy it is in the root folder,
beside `HELP.TXT`; on the live USB image it is in the `BORATYPE` folder.
Double-click it to start. The window fills most of the screen. You can resize
it, down to a small size where the layout gets compact, and **F6** shows it
full screen.

It needs about 164KB of free memory. It works on a CGA, EGA, VGA, VESA or
Hercules screen. The PC speaker plays the music and the effects. With an AdLib
or a Sound Blaster and the sound driver loaded, the music gets three FM voices
as well; the Control Panel's Sound page chooses where sound goes.

You can open more than one copy. Only the first one saves progress: the others
say **Read-only progress: another Borat session is open.** and show
**READ ONLY** at the bottom.

## The menu screen

The menu shows the profile in use and six buttons: **Continue lesson**,
**Choose lesson**, **Practice**, **Timed challenge**, **Profiles** and
**Records**. Continue lesson starts the first lesson you have not mastered. In
a large window the menu also shows the selected lesson, how many of the 24 you
have mastered, and your personal best speed.

## Profiles

There are four profiles, named `Player 1` to `Player 4` at first. Choose
**Profiles** (or press `N`), then click one or pick it with Up and Down. The
first key you type replaces its name; a name holds up to 12 characters, and
Backspace removes the last one. **Enter** keeps it and returns to the menu.

## Typing a lesson

Rest your left index finger on F and your right on J; you can feel the bumps
on those keys. The screen shows the text to type with the next character
highlighted, what you have typed, which finger to use, and a picture of the
keyboard coloured by finger. The key to press has an outline. A wrong key is
marked, and a shifted character also marks the Shift key on the other hand.

- Type the highlighted character exactly, with its capital, space or mark.
- A correct key moves on. A wrong key counts as an error and the target stays.
- **Backspace** takes back the last accepted character and ends your streak.
  Errors already made still count.
- Shift, Ctrl, Alt, function and arrow keys are never mistakes.
- Clicking a key on the picture only shows a hint. It types nothing.

The clock starts at the first key you press. **Accuracy** is correct key
presses out of all key presses. **Speed** is in words a minute, counting five
characters (spaces and marks included) as one word. A dash means there is
nothing to show yet.

A lesson ends when you reach the end of its text. Finishing at 95% accuracy or
better **masters** it. Every lesson can be chosen at any time, mastered or not.

| Lessons | What they teach |
|---|---|
| 1 to 6 | The home row: F and J, then D, K, S, L, A, semicolon, G and H, then words |
| 7 to 11 | The top row, then top and home together |
| 12 to 16 | The bottom row, up to every lowercase letter |
| 17 and 18 | Capitals with the left and the right Shift |
| 19 and 20 | The digits |
| 21 and 22 | Sentence marks and shifted symbols |
| 23 and 24 | Mixed sentences |

**Choose lesson** lists them all. A mastered lesson is marked `*` and a
finished one `+`. In a large window the list also gives your best accuracy for
each, and the right side shows the selected lesson's keys and its text. Up and
Down move the selection; **Enter** or **Start lesson** begins.

## Practice

Practice gives random words made from one group of keys: **Home row**, **All
letters**, **Digits** or **Mixed punctuation**. Press `1` to `4` or use Up and
Down to choose, then **Start practice** or **Enter**.

**Practice weak keys** (`W`) drills the keys you miss most often. A key needs
at least 20 tries before it can be called weak; until then the **TRY THESE
KEYS** box says **20 samples per key needed**, and practice uses the chosen
group.

## Timed challenges

Choose **1 minute**, **3 minute** or **5 minute** (or press `1` to `3`), a key
group with Up and Down, then **Start challenge**. A three-second **GET READY**
count comes first and is not timed. The challenge ends when its time is up,
even if you stop typing.

Pausing, switching to another window, moving or resizing the window, or going
full screen makes the challenge **unranked**. You can finish it, but it cannot
set a record.

## Results

The results show **SPEED**, **ACCURACY**, **ERRORS**, **BEST STREAK** and
**TIME**, your weakest keys, Borat's recent lines and any achievements the run
earned. The heading says **PERSONAL BEST!** for a challenge record,
**Completed**, **Practice / unranked**, or **No keys entered**. Press **Retry** (`R`), **Next
lesson** (`Enter`), **Weak keys** (`P`) or **Menu**.

## Pausing

**F4** or **Esc** pauses a lesson; the passage and score are kept. The pause
screen has **Resume (F4)**, **Restart**, **Display / Fullscreen** and **Exit
to menu**. **Enter** resumes, `R` restarts and `X` exits. Switching to
another window or resizing pauses too, and you resume when you are ready.
Help, Settings and Records opened during a lesson return to the pause screen.

## Records and achievements

**Records** shows your best speed and accuracy for each challenge length and
key group, how many lessons you have mastered, your best streak, and how many
minutes you have practised. There are seven achievements:

| Achievement | Earned by |
|---|---|
| First lesson | Finishing any lesson |
| 95% mastery | Finishing a lesson at 95% or better |
| 25-key streak | 25 correct keys in a row |
| 50-key streak | 50 correct keys in a row |
| 95% challenge | A ranked challenge at 95% or better |
| Perfect lesson | Finishing a lesson at 100% |
| Personal best | Beating your best speed |

## Sound and display

**Settings** (`S`) has these controls, with a key for each:

| Setting | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Music | `M` | The soundtrack on or off |
| Effects | `E` | The short sounds on or off |
| Key ticks | `K` | A tick on every key; off at first |
| Roast | `R` | How hard Borat teases: **Gentle**, **Classic** or **Extra** |
| Display | `D` | On a CGA, **Native** or **CGA color** |
| Test music | `T` | Plays the soundtrack (Music must be on) |

**Windowed** and **Full screen** switch the view. **Save settings** saves at
once. **F3** turns all sound off and on; the line at the bottom shows it.

On a CGA, **CGA color** makes **F6** open a 320x200 four-colour full screen,
and F6 brings you back. On other screens Display stays Native, and the screen
mode is set in the Control Panel.

## The menus

The **Borat Typing** menu has **About Borat Typing** and **Close**.

| Menu | Item | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| File | Profile... | `N` | The Profiles screen |
| File | Save progress | | Saves at once |
| File | Close | Ctrl+Q | Saves and closes, from full screen too |
| Lesson | Lessons | F2 | Choose lesson |
| Lesson | Practice | `P` | The Practice screen |
| Lesson | Challenge | `C` | The Timed challenge screen |
| Lesson | Records | `R` | The Records screen |
| View | Pause / Resume | F4 | Pauses or resumes a lesson |
| View | Full screen | F6 | Full screen and back; Alt+Enter too |
| View | Settings | `S` | Sound and display |
| Sound | Sound On / Off | F3 | All sound off or on |
| Sound | Test Music | | Plays the soundtrack |
| Help | Help | F1 | The help screen |

The letter keys work on the menu screens only. During a lesson every letter
is typing. Outside a lesson, **Esc** goes back a screen.

## Files

Progress and settings are saved in `BORATA.BTT` and `BORATB.BTT`, in
`SYSTEM\APPDATA` on the disk the program was started from. Each save goes to
the older of the two and is read back, so a failed write leaves the last good
copy. Saving happens at the end of a run, when you change profile or settings,
when you go back to the menu, and when you close. The first start says
**New profiles. Progress saves on this disk.**

## Good to know

- Keep the disk writable. If a save fails, the program says so and play goes
  on with the previous copy kept.
- The lessons use the US QWERTY layout.
- `HELP.TXT` on the floppy holds the same advice as the help screen.
