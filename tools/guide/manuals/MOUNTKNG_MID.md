# In the Hall of the Mountain King

Edvard Grieg's piece from Peer Gynt (1875). The theme comes round four
times, each time louder, higher and faster, the way the original builds: a
bassoon starts it, then strings, brass, a pizzicato bass, timpani and drums
join in. It plays for a little over a minute.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. This one opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

The music is Grieg's and in the public domain. The arrangement was written
for os8088, and the arrangement and this file are released into the public
domain under CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **MOUNTKNG.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it
from MIDIRack's playlist. The song changes speed as it goes, and MIDIRack's
**Tempo** line follows it. It plays on an FM chip, a Sound Blaster or the PC
speaker, whichever the machine has.
