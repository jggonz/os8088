# Sales sample

`SALES.SLK` is a small spreadsheet of a year's sales: the units sold each
month from January to December, and the revenue, worked out by a formula
as the units times 25. Beside the table it totals the units and the
revenue, finds the best month and the average.

![Sheet with the sample sales figures open.](shot:sheet-window)

## Where it is

In the `MEDIA` folder of the Office disk, and on the live USB image and CD.

## Using it

Double-click **SALES.SLK** and Sheet ([[SHEET.O88]]) opens with the figures. Click
a revenue cell to see its formula on the formula line, or change a month's
units and watch its revenue and the totals change. **Data > Chart
Column...** charts the column the cursor is in.

It is also the sample for Chart ([[CHART.O88]]). A double-click opens Sheet, so
start Chart and open this file with **File > Open...** to chart the monthly
units.

The file is in SYLK format, a plain-text spreadsheet format that Sheet
saves and that other spreadsheet programs read.
