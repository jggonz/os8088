# Flower

A close-up of a sunflower in full bloom against a background of green
leaves. It is a 640 by 480 picture stored as a JPEG, with its colour kept
at full detail, which makes the file a little larger than Vacation's.

![Flower open in PiXEL, fitted to the window.](shot:flower-jpg)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB sizes. The 360KB PiXEL disk leaves it out for room.

## Opening it

Double-click **FLOWER.JPG** and PiXEL ([[PIXEL.O88]]) opens with the picture, fitted to the
window. **1:1** on the toolbar shows it at its own size, and `F` shows it
on the whole screen. Press `Space` for the next picture in the folder.
