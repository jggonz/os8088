# Demo battle

`BATTLE.VBT` is the demo battle that Video Battle ([[VBATTLE.O88]]) plays.
Two heroes, ARI and BRAM, face a giant BEETLE in front of a city skyline on
a glowing grid. Everything in it was drawn and scored
for this project: the picture is made by a program, not filmed, and the music
and sounds are its own.

![The demo battle at its first menu.](shot:vbattle-menu)

## What is inside it

- A title, **VIDEO BATTLE**, that goes round until you press `Enter`.
- An opening that reveals the scene, then the battle's music, which loops.
- ARI's **Attack**, **Bolt** and **Limit**, BRAM's **Fire** and **Heal**,
  and the BEETLE's **Lunge**, each a clip with its own sounds that joins the
  scene and returns to it.
- A victory clip that fades to a closing scene, and a defeat that fades back
  to the title to play again.

## Why it is on these disks, and which one is which

It is on every Video Battle disk, beside the program, so there is a battle
to play. The disks carry different versions:

| File | On | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `BATTLE.VBT` | the 1.44MB and 1.2MB disks | the whole battle, for CGA |
| `BATTLE.VBT` | the 720KB and 360KB disks | a shorter battle for a floppy: the title plays once instead of waiting for a key, ARI has no Limit, the music is two bars, short enough to be held in memory, and the battle ends on its last picture |
| `BATTLEV.VBT` | the VGA disk | the whole battle in eight VGA colours |
| `BATTLEH.VBT` | the Hercules disk | the whole battle for a Hercules card |

Video Battle picks the version for your screen when they sit side by side,
so you can copy all three into one folder.

## Playing it

Double-click **BATTLE.VBT** and Video Battle opens with it; press `Enter` to
play. The [[VBATTLE.O88]] page has the keys. The 1.44MB and 1.2MB battle
streams its music and clips from the disk, so it plays best copied to a hard
disk with the program.
