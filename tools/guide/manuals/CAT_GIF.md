# Cat

A tabby cat looking straight at you, sitting in a softly lit living room.
It is a 320 by 240 GIF picture in 256 colours.

![The cat open in PiXEL.](shot:cat-gif)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in all four sizes.

## Opening it

A double-click on a GIF file starts Paint ([[PAINT.O88]]), which cannot open a GIF as
large as this one: its window opens with an empty canvas, and the menu bar
says **GIF larger than 64KB** for a few seconds. Open the cat in
PiXEL instead. Either double-click another picture in the folder, such as
`BALLOONS.PNG`, and press `Space` until the cat appears, or start PiXEL and
choose **File > Open...**.

PiXEL shows it at its own size with its own 256-colour palette, which you
can see in the information pane on the right.
