# Hello

Hello is the smallest os8088 program: one window that says **Hello from a
.o88 package!**, a File menu that changes what it says, and an About card.
It does nothing else. It is there as an example for people who write
programs for os8088, who can read its source to see the least a windowed
program needs.

## Starting Hello

Hello is in the `APPS` folder of the live USB stick and CD, and on no
floppy. Double-click **HELLO.O88**. It has no icon of its own, so the Disk
window shows the plain program icon for it. You can open more than one
Hello; each window keeps its own page.

## Using Hello

The window shows two lines of text, centred. While a Hello window is in
front, the menu bar reads **Hello** and **File**.

Choose **File > About** and the two lines change to **HELLO - the smallest
os8088 package.** Choose **File > Greeting** to go back to the first two
lines.

## The Hello menu

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| About Hello | Shows the About card, with who made Hello. Click the window or pick a menu item to put it away |
| Close | Closes this Hello |

## The File menu

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Shows **Hello from a .o88 package!** |
| About | Shows **HELLO - the smallest os8088 package.** |

## Good to know

- Hello has no keys, opens no files and saves nothing.
- A click in the window does nothing unless the About card is up, when it
  puts the card away.
- The window cannot be resized.
