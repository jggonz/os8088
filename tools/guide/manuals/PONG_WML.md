# Pong markup

`PONG.WML` lays out Weave Pong: a title, the score, a black playing field
240 by 120 pixels with a yellow ball and two paddles (yours on the left in
light cyan, the computer's on the right in light magenta), and a **Serve**
button. The top and bottom of the field are walls; a ball that leaves by
either side is a point.

![The Pong app these files make, running in Weave.](shot:weave-pong)

## Where it is

In the `LOOM` folder of the Weave disk and the Loom disk, in every size,
and of the second disk of the everything set. The compiled app it belongs
to is in the `WEAVE` folder beside it.

## Using it

Double-click **PONG.WML** and Loom ([[LOOM.O88]]) opens the Pong project, with
[[PONG.WJS]] and [[PONG.WSP]] beside it in the list. Change a colour or
the field's size here, then pack the project to make a new app. The packed
result of these sources is [[PONG.WAB]].
