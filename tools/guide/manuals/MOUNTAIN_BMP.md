# Mountain

A green alpine valley with a winding path and stream, dark pine forest on
both sides, and snow-capped peaks under a cloudy sky. It is a 256 by 192
Windows bitmap (BMP) in full colour, stored without compression.

![Mountain open in PiXEL.](shot:mountain-bmp)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB sizes. The 360KB PiXEL disk leaves it out for room.

## Opening it

A double-click on a BMP file starts Paint ([[PAINT.O88]]), which is a drawing program with
16 colours. To see the picture in full colour, open it in PiXEL: double-click
another picture in the folder and press `Space` until the mountain appears,
or start PiXEL and choose **File > Open...**.
