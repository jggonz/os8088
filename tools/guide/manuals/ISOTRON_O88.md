# isoTron

isoTron is a light-wall duel: you against the computer on a raised slab
seen from above at an angle. Each of you trails a wall that never goes
away. Run into any wall, yours or the computer's, or off the edge of the
slab, and the round is lost. Make the computer crash first and you win it.

Win two rounds running and the next arena is stacked one slab higher, with
one more computer opponent to beat. Past four slabs the tower starts again
from one. Three losses in a row end the game.

![A round in progress: your wall comes up from the front of the slab, the computer's from the back.](shot:isotron-window){full}

## Starting isoTron

isoTron is in the `GAMES` folder of the software disk, and at the top level
of the games disk. Double-click **ISOTRON.O88**. The window opens on the
title screen, which lists the keys.

![The title screen. Press 1, 2 or 3 to start.](shot:isotron-title){full}

Pick how hard the computer plays:

| Key | Game |
|---|---|
| `1` | Rookie, the pace of the original 1982 game |
| `2` | Veteran, a third faster |
| `3` | Lunatic, twice as fast |
| `A`, `B`, `C` | The same three games with the sound off |

The **Game** menu starts the same three games, with sound.

## Playing

You start at the front of the slab heading away from you. The computer
starts at the back. Three beeps count you in, then both walls start to
grow.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Ins`, `Left` or `Z` | Turn left |
| `Del`, `Right` or `X` | Turn right |
| `F10` or `Up` | Go faster. Each press adds a little, up to four and a half times normal speed. Holding the key keeps speeding up |
| `F9` or `Down` | Back to normal speed |
| `P` | Pause, and press again to carry on |
| `M` | Sound on or off |
| `C` | Change the colours (see below) |
| `F` or `Alt+Enter` | Full screen, and back to the window |
| `Space` or `Enter` | Carry on after a round |
| `Esc` | End the game |

Holding a turn key turns you once. To turn round on the spot, tap the same
turn key twice quickly. Speed is a weapon: the computer moves a little
slower while you are boosted, but you have less time to turn.

When someone crashes, the score goes up for the winner and the window says
**Player wins.** or **Computer wins.** Press `Space` to go on to the next
round. The new arena is swept onto the screen, rims first, in about a
second and a half.

![The computer has boxed itself in. The player scores.](shot:isotron-crash){full}

## Score and the tower

The winner of each round scores one point. **PLAYER** is shown at the top
left and **COMPUTER** at the bottom right. There is no winning score: the
game goes on until you lose three rounds in a row, and then the screen says
**THREE LOSSES IN A ROW. GAME OVER.**

The tower grows only when you win two rounds running. Each new slab adds a
computer opponent, up to four of them at once. A loss never takes a slab
away, and after the fourth slab the tower starts again at one. More
opponents slow everyone down; each one that crashes speeds the game up
again.

## Full screen

Press `F`, `Alt+Enter`, or choose **Game > Full Screen** to play the same
game on the whole screen, in colour where the display has it. Press `F`
again to go back to the window. At the title screen in full screen, `Esc`
also goes back to the window.

![The same game full screen on a VGA display.](shot:isotron-full){full}

On a CGA display, full screen uses the four colours of the 1982 original.
VGA and EGA show sixteen colours, and Hercules shows black-and-white
patterns. In a window, CGA and Hercules use patterns too.

## Colours

On a colour display, `C` during a game, or **Game > Colours**, switches
between the isoTron colours and Classic, the four colours of the original.
On a CGA display in full screen it switches the brighter version of the
colours on and off. On a black-and-white display the item reads
**Colours (mono display)** and is greyed.

## The menus

| Menu | Item | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| isoTron | About isoTron | Shows the name and the main keys |
| isoTron | Close | Closes isoTron |
| Game | Rookie, Veteran, Lunatic | Starts a new game at that level, with sound |
| Game | Sound: On / Sound: Off | Turns the sound off or on. The label shows the sound as it is now |
| Game | Colours | Switches the colours, like `C` |
| Game | Full Screen | Full screen, like `F`. It reads **Full Screen (no mode)** and is greyed on a display that has no full-screen mode for it |
| Game | Pause | Pauses the game, like `P` |
| Game | End Game | Ends the game and goes back to the title |

## Sound

isoTron plays through the PC speaker: a soft tick as your wall grows,
higher while you are boosted, a falling sweep at every crash, three beeps
before each round, a chime as the tower rises, and four notes when the game
ends. `M` turns the sound off and on.

## Good to know

- isoTron keeps the original game's pace on any PC, from a 4.77 MHz XT to a
  fast 386. A faster computer does not make the game faster.
- If you click another window during a round, the game pauses itself. Press
  `P` or `Space` to carry on.
- It needs about 93 KB of free memory besides its own 21 KB, so a 256 KB
  machine can run it. If there is not enough, the window says how much it
  needs and how much the machine has.
- If the window is made too small for the arena, it says **Window too
  small.**
- The scores are not saved when you close the game.
