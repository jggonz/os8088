# Calculator

Calculator is a four-function desk calculator. It keeps the last eight sums
you worked out in a history list that folds away under the keypad. Click any
line of the history and its answer comes back into the display, ready to be
the start of the next sum.

![The Calculator in the middle of a sum.](shot:calc-window){full}

## Starting Calculator

Open the disk that holds it, then the `APPS` folder, and double-click
**CALC.O88**. You can open more than one Calculator at a time; each keeps its
own display and history.

## Working out a sum

Click the keys with the mouse, or type them. Every key on the keypad has a
key on the keyboard as well:

| On the keypad | On the keyboard | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `0` to `9`, `.` | the same keys | Enter a number |
| `+` `-` `*` `/` | the same keys, or `x` for times | Choose the operation |
| `=` | `=` or `Enter` | Show the answer |
| `<-` | `Backspace` | Remove the last digit you typed |
| `CE` | `E` | Clear the number you are typing, and keep the sum |
| `C` | `C` or `Esc` | Clear everything and start again |
| `+/-` | `N` | Change the sign of the number |

If you press two operations in a row, such as `2 +` then `*`, the second one
wins and nothing is worked out yet.

The display holds nine digits, with up to eight after the point. A tenth
digit simply does not appear. When an answer is too big to show, the display
says **Error**; press `C` or `CE` to carry on.

## The Math menu

| Item | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Square Root | `Q` | The square root of the number in the display |
| Reciprocal | `R` | One divided by the number |
| Percent | `%` | See below |
| Negate | `N` | Changes the sign, like the `+/-` key |

**Percent** works the way a desk calculator's does. After `+` or `-` it adds
or takes away that percentage, so `200 + 15 %` gives 230. After `*` or `/`,
or on its own, it turns the number into a hundredth: `15 %` is 0.15.

## The history

![The history open, with two sums in it.](shot:calc-history){right}

The history shows your last eight sums, newest at the top. Click the
**History** strip under the keypad, press `H`, or choose **View > Show
History** to open and close it.

Click a line and its answer goes into the display, so you can keep working
from it. **View > Clear History** empties the list.

## Copying numbers in and out

**Edit > Copy** (`Ctrl-C`) puts the number in the display on the clipboard,
where [[NOTEPAD.O88]], [[TEXPAD.O88]] and the other programs can paste it. The menu bar
briefly shows **Copied**.

**Edit > Paste** (`Ctrl-V`) types the clipboard into the Calculator as if
you had pressed the keys yourself. It stops at the first character the
keypad does not have.

## Good to know

- Calculator has no memory keys. Use the history instead: it holds eight
  answers, each one labelled with the sum it came from.
- Calculator does not save anything to disk. To keep a number, copy it into
  a document.
- **Calc > About Calc** shows who made it. **Calc > Close** closes this
  Calculator.
