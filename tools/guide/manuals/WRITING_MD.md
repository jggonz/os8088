# Writing in ArtfulType

`WRITING.MD` is a short guide to ArtfulType that is also a demonstration of
it. It is written in Markdown, the plain-text way of marking headings, bold,
italic, code, strike-through and links, and ArtfulType shows each mark as
the style it stands for while you type.

![WRITING.MD in ArtfulType's writing screen, styled as you type.](shot:artful-writer)

## Where it is

In the `MEDIA` folder of the Office disk, and on the live USB image and CD.
Its words are shown below.

## Opening it

Double-click **WRITING.MD** and ArtfulType ([[ARTFUL.O88]]) opens straight into the
writing screen with the guide loaded. Press `Esc` to hand the screen back
to the desktop; the document is still there when you return. The guide
explains the two views of the same file, Writer and Markdown, and the
keys for each mark.
