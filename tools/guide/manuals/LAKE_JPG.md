# Lake photo

A still mountain lake at dawn in black and white: pine woods on both
shores, mist on the water, peaks reflected in it and a small wooden jetty
in the foreground. It is a 320 by 240 greyscale JPEG.

![The lake picture open in PiXEL, at its own size.](shot:lake-jpg)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk in all four sizes, and in the
`MEDIA` folder of the Office disk and the live USB image and CD, so that
PiXEL has a picture to open there too.

## Opening it

Double-click **LAKE.JPG** and PiXEL ([[PIXEL.O88]]) opens with the picture at its own size.
A greyscale picture looks the same on every screen PiXEL works on, and on
a black-and-white CGA or Hercules screen it is the sample that shows the
best. It is also the smallest file of the ten, so it opens quickest.
