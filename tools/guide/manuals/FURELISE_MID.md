# Fur Elise

Beethoven's Bagatelle in A minor, known as Fur Elise, written in 1810. This
arrangement is for piano, right hand and left hand, at 112 beats a minute,
and plays for about 43 seconds.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, opened by a double-click.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

The melody is Beethoven's, and long out of copyright. The arrangement was
written for os8088, and the arrangement and this file are released into
the public domain under CC0. MIDIRack's **MIDI Info** card shows the
copyright line stored in the song.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **FURELISE.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it. MIDIRack also
lists it with the other nine songs whenever it opens with nothing to play.
It plays through whatever sound hardware the machine has: an AdLib or Sound
Blaster FM chip, the Sound Blaster's own sound, or the PC speaker.
