# Loom

The `LOOM` folder holds Loom, the editor for Weave apps, and the source files
of the three demo apps:

- `LOOM.O88`, the editor;
- the demo apps' sources: `FORM.WML` and `FORM.WJS`; `SHEET.WML`,
  `SHEET.WJS` and `SHEET.WFX`; `PONG.WML`, `PONG.WJS` and `PONG.WSP`. Each
  app's files share its name: its page, its script, and for some its
  formulas or sprites.

## Where it is

On the Loom disk and the Weave disk, in all four sizes, beside the `WEAVE`
folder, and on the everything set and the live USB image and CD.

## Using it

Double-click a `.WML` or `.WJS` file to open that app in Loom. When you pack
an app, Loom writes its `.WAB` into this folder beside the sources, and the
copies in `WEAVE` stay as they were. A new app of your own is tidiest in a
folder of its own.
