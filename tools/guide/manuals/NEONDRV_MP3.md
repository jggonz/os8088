# Neon Drive

A ten-second original piece made for XTMP3, in stereo. It is stored at
44,100 samples a second, the rate of a CD, at 56 kbps.

## Where it comes from

It was written for os8088 by a program that composes its notes, and encoded
to MP3. No recording or existing song is in it.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA` on the 1.44MB software disk, on XTMP3's own disk in every size,
and on the live USB image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **NEONDRV.MP3** and XTMP3 ([[XTMP3.O88]]) opens and plays it.
It is the demo song that asks the most of a computer: a fast one plays it
in stereo at its own rate, a slower one at half or a quarter of the rate,
and an XT decodes it to the hard disk first.
