# Word

The `WORD` folder holds Microsoft Word and its welcome document:

- `WORD.O88`, the word processor;
- `WELCOME.DOC`, a document that shows off its formatting. Double-click it
  to read it.

## Where it is

This folder is on the everything set and on the live USB image and CD. On
Word's own disk the same two files are at the top level of the disk, beside
an empty `DOCS` folder for your work.

Typefaces are not kept here: Word finds them in `SYSTEM/FONTS` on the system
disk.
