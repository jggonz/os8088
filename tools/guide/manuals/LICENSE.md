# Licence

`LICENSE` is the licence of RunCPM, the program the CP/M emulator is built
from. It is the MIT License, which lets anyone use, copy, change and share
the program as long as this notice goes with it. Its words are shown
below.

## Where it is

At the top of the CP/M emulator disk in every size, beside `RUNCPM.O88`,
and in the `RUNCPM` folder of the everything set. It has no extension, as
it has in RunCPM's own source; Note Pad opens it with **File > Open...**.

## Why it is there

RunCPM was written by Marcelo Dantas, who signs the licence as "Mockba the
Borg", and the CP/M emulator for os8088 is a port of it. The MIT License
asks for this notice to be included with every copy, so it travels on the
disk with the program.
