# Room

A teenager's computer corner from the 1980s: a beige PC with its monitor
and speakers on a wooden desk, a lamp, shelves of books, and posters
covering the wall. It is a 640 by 480 picture stored as a progressive
JPEG, the kind a web page shows in rough form first and then sharpens.

![Room open in PiXEL. It is read at a quarter of its size and fitted to the window.](shot:room-jpg)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB sizes. The 360KB PiXEL disk leaves it out for room.

## Opening it

Double-click **ROOM.JPG** and PiXEL ([[PIXEL.O88]]) opens with the picture. A progressive
JPEG takes PiXEL longer to read than an ordinary one, and on a 4.77 MHz
XT the window stays empty for a while with **Opening** and a percentage
in the status bar before the picture appears. PiXEL reads it at a quarter
of its size, which fits the window. Press `Space` for the next picture in
the folder.
