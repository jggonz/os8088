# Sheet formulas

`SHEET.WFX` holds the Picnic Budget's starting cells, one per line: the
labels **Item**, **Cost** and **Note**, three items with their costs (bread
3.50, cheese 7.25 and cider 12), and four formulas. They give the total
with `SUM`, the total rounded with `ROUND`, the average with `AVG`, and
whether the picnic is cheap with `IF` (1 when the total is under 25).

![The Picnic Budget app, with the total and the average worked out from these formulas.](shot:weave-sheet)

## Where it is

In the `LOOM` folder of the Weave disk and the Loom disk, in every size,
and of the second disk of the everything set. The compiled app it belongs
to is in the `WEAVE` folder beside it.

## Using it

A double-click does not open it. Open the project's [[SHEET.WML]] in
[[LOOM.O88]] and pick **SHEET.WFX** in the list to edit it there. Each line
is a cell name, `=`, and a number, a label in quotes, or a formula that
starts with another `=`.
