# Licence

`COPYING` is the GNU General Public License, version 2, the licence of the
two emulators on os8088 that are built from VICE and other free emulators:
the Commodore 64 and the Apple II Plus. It lets anyone use, share and
change those programs, as long as what they pass on stays under the same
licence with its source available. Its words are shown below.

## Where it is

There are two versions, one beside each program:

- In the `C64` folder of the Commodore 64 disk and the everything set: the
  GNU General Public License, version 2, on its own.
- In the `APPLE2` folder of the Apple II Plus disk and the everything set:
  the same licence, followed by the notices of the other emulators parts
  of it come from: the MIT notices of MII (Michel Pollet) and of a second
  emulator by Mark Allender, and the copyright lines of AppleWin.

## Why it is there

The rest of os8088 is shared under its own terms. These two programs are
not, because they are made from programs under the GPL, and the GPL asks
that every copy carry the licence. Keep `COPYING` in the folder if you
copy the program to another disk. It has no extension; Note Pad opens it
with **File > Open...**.
