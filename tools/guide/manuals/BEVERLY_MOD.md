# Beverly Hills Cop

The theme from the 1984 film Beverly Hills Cop, Harold Faltermeyer's "Axel
F", as an Amiga music module. A module carries its own instrument sounds
inside it, so it sounds the same wherever it plays: this one has fifteen
of them, among them brass, synth brass, drums, toms and hi-hats, playing in
four channels at 125 beats a minute.

![Tracker playing Beverly Hills Cop in its window.](shot:tracker-window)

## Where it comes from

It is a four-channel ProTracker module of the kind traded on the Amiga
and the PC demo scene. The module's own instrument list credits Daniel
Berlin. It is the module Tracker was written and tested with.

## Where it is

In the `MEDIA` folder of the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the
first disk of the everything set, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD. It does not fit on the 360KB software disk beside the
programs, so at that size it has a disk of its own, the music disk, again
in `MEDIA`.

## Playing it

Double-click **BEVERLY.MOD** and Tracker ([[TRACKER.O88]]) opens and plays it. Tracker reads
the whole module into memory first, which takes a few seconds from a
floppy. It plays through a Sound Blaster; with no card it plays through
the PC speaker, or through a Covox if one is chosen on the Control Panel's
**Sound** page.

Tracker's window shows the song's name, the position and pattern it is
playing, and the speed. **Full Scrn** shows the channels on the whole
screen.
