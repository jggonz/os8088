# Dot Delirium

Dot Delirium is a maze chase in the Pac-Man tradition. You steer Smiles round
the maze eating dots while four ghosts chase you. Eat a power pellet and the
ghosts turn blue and run, and for a short while you can eat them. Clear every
dot to move on to the next board. It plays in a window or full screen.

![A game in progress in the window, with the score panel on the left.](shot:dotdel-play){full}

## Starting Dot Delirium

![The title screen, with the high scores and the demo game.](shot:dotdel-title){left}

Dot Delirium is on the games disk, at the top level, and in the `GAMES`
folder of the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks. Double-click
**DOTDEL.O88**. It needs nothing special and plays on VGA, EGA, Hercules and
CGA.

The window opens on the title screen: the name, the high-score table and a
demo game playing underneath. Press `Enter` or `Space`, or click in the
window, to start a game.

## Playing

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys, or `W` `A` `S` `D` | Steer Smiles |
| `P` | Pause, and press again to carry on |
| `F` or `Alt+Enter` | Play full screen, and back |
| `Esc` | Leave full screen. In the window, give up the game and go back to the title |

Smiles remembers the last direction you pressed and turns that way at the
next opening, so you can press a turn before you reach it. The passage across the middle of the maze runs off one side and
comes back on the other.

Each life begins with **READY!**. You have three lives. A ghost that catches
Smiles costs one, and when the last one goes the board says **GAME OVER**.

A power pellet turns all four ghosts blue and sends them the other way. Eat
a blue ghost and its eyes go back to the ghost house, and the ghost comes
out again. On later levels the ghosts are faster and stay blue for less time.

## Scoring

| What you eat | Points |
|---|---|
| A dot | 10 |
| A power pellet | 50 |
| Blue ghosts from one pellet | 200, 400, 800 and 1,600 |
| A fruit | 100 at first, rising to 5,000 on later levels |

A fruit appears under the ghost house twice on each board and waits about
nine seconds to be eaten. At 10,000 points you get one extra life, once a
game.

## Levels and boards

When the last dot is eaten the board shows **LEVEL CLEAR** and the next
level starts. There are three different mazes, and the maze changes every
three levels: the classic board, then one with long halls, then one with
combs and an open middle, and round again.

## Full screen

![Full screen on VGA. Two ghosts have turned blue after a power pellet.](shot:dotdel-fullscreen){full}

Press `F` or choose **Game > Full Screen** and the board fills the whole
screen, on the same display as the window. On a CGA that makes the board much
bigger. Press `F` or `Esc` to go back to the window.

## The menus

| Menu | Item | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Game | New Game | Start a new game |
| Game | Pause | Pause or carry on, like `P` |
| Game | Full Screen | Play full screen, like `F` |
| Game | Sound | Turn the sound effects off, and on again |
| Window | Thin | A narrower board, with the window shrunk to fit it |
| Window | Full | A wider board in a wider window |
| Dot Delirium | About Dot Delirium | The keys and who made it |
| Dot Delirium | Close | Close the game |

![The About card. Click in the window or press a key to put it away.](shot:dotdel-about){right}

**Thin** and **Full** choose the shape of the board, and which looks right
depends on your monitor. The window starts in Thin. On a CGA display the
board is always the full shape, and Thin is greyed out with the reason
**(200 lines)**. Full screen always uses the full shape.

The sound effects are simple tones and need no sound card. They are on
when the game opens.

While the About card is up the game stands still. Click in the window or
press any key to put it away; that click or key does nothing else.

## High scores

The table keeps six scores, each with three initials. Until a score has been
saved, it shows six built-in entries. When your score beats the bottom row, a panel
appears on the board: type up to three letters, use `Backspace` to correct
them, and press `Enter`. The table is saved straight away as `DOTDEL.HS` in
the `SYSTEM\APPDATA` folder of the disk Dot Delirium was started from, so the
disk must not be write-protected.

## Good to know

- If the window is too small for the board, it says **Window too small.**
  If the computer has too little free memory, it says **Not enough
  memory.**
- Dot Delirium is for one player, and the mouse only starts a game; it does
  not steer.
