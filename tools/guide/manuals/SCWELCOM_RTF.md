# Scribe welcome

`SCWELCOM.RTF` is the welcome document on Scribe's disk, saved as an RTF
file, Scribe's own format. It says what Scribe adds to Word - pictures -
and how its two file formats treat them, then runs through bold, italic and
underlined text, small capitals, a hanging indent, centred and flush-right
lines, a double-spaced paragraph and a table of useful keys.

![Scribe with SCWELCOM.RTF open.](shot:scribe-window)

## Where it is

At the top of the Scribe disk in every size, beside [[SCWELCOM.DOC]], and in
the `SCRIBE` folder of the live USB image and CD.

## Opening it

Start Scribe ([[SCRIBE.O88]]) and choose **File > Open...**, then pick
**SCWELCOM.RTF**. Scribe never takes over a double-click, so open your
documents from inside it.
