# RealDMG demo

`GBDEMO.GB` is a Game Boy cartridge made by the os8088 project for
[[REALDMG.O88]], the Game Boy program. It is the only cartridge that comes
with RealDMG, so there is always something to run. It was written for an IBM
PC 5150, the slowest machine os8088 runs on, and it runs on a real Game Boy
too.

![The demo running in RealDMG's window.](shot:realdmg-window){full}

## What it shows

A still screen with the title **REALDMG DEMO**, three lines of text, a band
of the Game Boy's four shades and a strip of ground. A ball bounces along the
ground from wall to wall, and a clock under it counts the minutes and seconds
since the demo started. Each time the ball lands it makes a short blip.

There is nothing to play: the demo takes no buttons. It is for seeing that
RealDMG, the picture, the timing and the sound work on your machine.

## Where it is

In the `REALDMG` folder of RealDMG's disk in every size, beside
`REALDMG.O88` and `README.TXT`, and in the same folder on the everything set
and the live USB image and CD.

## Running it

Double-click **GBDEMO.GB** and RealDMG opens and starts it. Or open
RealDMG and choose **Machine > Load Game...**.

The demo was made to need very little, so it runs at full Game Boy speed
even on a 4.77 MHz 8088. There RealDMG draws about four pictures a second.
Press `F1` to see the speed line, and `Alt+Enter` to watch it on the whole
screen. With a Sound Blaster or an AdLib the blip plays on the card;
otherwise it plays on the PC speaker.
