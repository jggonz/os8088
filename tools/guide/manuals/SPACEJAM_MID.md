# Space Jam

**Orbital Groove**, original music in the style of a 1990s computer game: a
bouncing synth bass, a synth lead, chord stabs and a pad of synth strings
over a steady beat, at 118 beats a minute. It plays for about a minute.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. Space Jam opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

It is an original composition, made for os8088 and quoting no existing
tune. Its name is the file's, and it has nothing to do with any film. The
music and this file are released into the public domain under CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **SPACEJAM.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it
from MIDIRack's playlist. It plays on an FM chip, a Sound Blaster or the PC
speaker, whichever the machine has.
