# Korobeiniki

The Russian folk song Korobeiniki, first published as a poem in 1861 and
known to many people as the music of Tetris. This version is in A minor: a
square-wave lead with a harmony part, an octave-jumping synth bass, strings
and drums, at 144 beats a minute. It plays for about 42 seconds.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. Korobeiniki opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

The tune is a traditional folk song in the public domain. The arrangement
was written for os8088, and the arrangement and this file are released into
the public domain under CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **KOROBEIN.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it
from MIDIRack's playlist. It plays on an FM chip, a Sound Blaster or the PC
speaker, whichever the machine has.
