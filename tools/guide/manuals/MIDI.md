# MIDI songs

`MIDI` holds ten demo songs for MIDIRack, as Standard MIDI Files: Fur Elise,
The Entertainer, the Can-can, the Turkish March, In the Hall of the Mountain
King, Korobeiniki, and four original pieces, a fanfare, two pieces of game
music and a demo of MIDI effects.

## Where it is

Inside `MEDIA`, as `MEDIA/MIDI`: all ten songs on the 1.44MB and 1.2MB
software disks, the music disk and MIDIRack's own disk; the 720KB software
disk has room for two of them, BATTLE1.MID and INTRO.MID.

## Using it

Double-click any song to open MIDIRack and play it. Opened on its own,
MIDIRack looks in `MEDIA/MIDI` and fills its playlist from this folder, so
the songs are ready to play straight away. It plays through whatever sound
hardware the machine has, down to the PC speaker.

You can add your own `.MID` files here and they join the playlist the next
time MIDIRack opens.
