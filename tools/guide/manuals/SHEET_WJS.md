# Sheet script

`SHEET.WJS` is the Picnic Budget's script. Clicking a cell shows its row,
column and value on the status line, and after each recalculation the line
says how many cells changed. **Show total** shows the total in an alert,
as a whole number, and **Cider +1** adds one to the cost of the cider so
you can watch the totals follow.

![The Picnic Budget app these files make, running in Weave.](shot:weave-sheet)

## Where it is

In the `LOOM` folder of the Weave disk and the Loom disk, in every size,
and of the second disk of the everything set. The compiled app it belongs
to is in the `WEAVE` folder beside it.

## Using it

Double-click **SHEET.WJS** and Loom ([[LOOM.O88]]) opens the project; click
**SHEET.WJS** in the list on the left to edit the script. The packed app is [[SHEET.WAB]].
