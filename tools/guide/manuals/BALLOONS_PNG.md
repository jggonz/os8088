# Balloons

A sky full of striped hot-air balloons over green hills, with a crowd and
tents at a fair below. It is a 320 by 240 PNG picture in 256 colours, drawn
in a flat, cartoon-like style.

![Balloons open in PiXEL.](shot:balloons-png)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in all four sizes.

## Opening it

Double-click **BALLOONS.PNG** and PiXEL ([[PIXEL.O88]]) opens with the picture at its own
size. Its name comes first in the folder, so it is the picture to start
from when you want to step through the whole set with `Space`.
