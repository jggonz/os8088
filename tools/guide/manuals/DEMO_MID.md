# MIDI demo

**MIDIRack Feature Test**, a 45-second piece written to try out everything a
MIDI player has to get right. Play it when you set up a new sound card, or
to hear what each of MIDIRack's outputs can do.

![MIDIRack playing the MIDI demo, with its first instrument showing in the Channel Rack.](shot:midirack-window)

## What you hear

It runs through nine short sections, one after another:

1. Ten different instruments on one channel, two beats each.
2. A note bent up two semitones and back, then down and back.
3. The modulation wheel turned up over one note and down over the next.
4. The tempo slowing to 90, and a string chord swelling in volume.
5. A brass chord dipping and rising in expression.
6. Three beats to the bar, with an organ swept from left to right.
7. Short notes held on by the sustain pedal.
8. Every General MIDI drum sound in turn, at 140 beats a minute.
9. Everything together, and a last chord.

Watch MIDIRack's Channel Rack while it plays: the instrument names change
as the sections go by.

## Where it comes from

It was written for os8088. The music and this file are released into the
public domain under CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **DEMO.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it from
MIDIRack's playlist. The PC speaker plays only the tune, so the effects are
best heard on an FM chip or a Sound Blaster.
