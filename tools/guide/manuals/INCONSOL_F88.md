# Inconsolata

Inconsolata is a compact fixed-width typeface: every character is the same
width, and that width is only 6 pixels. A line of it holds 106 characters
across a 640-pixel screen, where Courier holds 80. It is the face for wide
tables, program listings and anything that needs many columns in one window.

![Font Viewer showing Inconsolata with a sample typed in.](shot:inconsol-f88)

## Where it comes from

This face was fitted for os8088 onto the screen's grid from Inconsolata, by
the Inconsolata Project Authors, drawn at a semi-bold weight so that its
strokes stay solid.

Inconsolata is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and so is this face.
`LICENSE.TXT` in the same folder carries the licence and the authors' credit.

## What is in it

One style of 95 characters: the capital and small letters, the figures and
the punctuation of plain ASCII. There are no accented letters. Every
character is 6 pixels wide, and each line is 12 pixels tall with no extra
space between lines.

## Using it

The face lives in `SYSTEM/FONTS` on the system disk. Double-click
**INCONSOL.F88** to look at it in Font Viewer.

Microsoft Word, the C edition of Word and Scribe list it in the **Font** box
on their ribbon as **Inconsol**. Pick it and the whole document is shown in
it; the ribbon then names it in full, **Inconsolata**.
