# Welcome note

`WELCOME.TXT` is the first note on the Sticky Note pad ([[STIKNOTE.O88]]).
It is a short note that shows how the pad works: its first line is the
title, and three of its lines are a checklist, one already ticked. Its words
are shown below.

![The welcome note on the Sticky Note pad.](shot:stiknote-welcome)

## Where it is

In the [[NOTES]] folder, inside `SYSTEM\APPDATA` on the system disk in every
size, on the emulator system disk and on the live USB image and CD. Every
copy is the same.

## Using it

Open Sticky Note and the pad shows this note. Click its boxes to tick them,
or click the arrow at the bottom of the sheet to open it in Note Pad. In
Note Pad the boxes show as `[ ]` and `[x]`.

It is an ordinary note: change it, or delete it from the pad with `Del`
pressed twice. A deleted welcome note does not come back.
