# Catalog

`CATALOG.TXT` says what is in the place it is in, and how to open it. Two
different files have this name. Its words are shown below, per disk.

## On the Weave disk

The guide to the Weave disk: what is in the `WEAVE` and `LOOM` folders,
what each of the three demo apps shows off, which source files each was
built from, and how to run, edit and repack them. It is at the top of the
Weave disk in every size. The copies differ only in their last line, which
names the size of the disk.

Double-click it to read it in Note Pad. The short version: open the
`WEAVE` folder and double-click a `.WAB` to run an app, or open the `LOOM`
folder and double-click a `.WML` to edit one in Loom, where `^P` packs a
new bundle beside the sources.

## On the live USB stick and CD

The catalogue of the story library, in the `STORIES` folder. For each story
in `INFOCOM`, `CLASSIC` and `MODERN` it gives the file name, the title, the
author, the story's version and its size, and a line about it. It ends with
how much memory a story needs, and with the Infocom games that are not
there because they are still sold.

Double-click it to read it in Note Pad, and double-click a story to play it
in [[FROTZ.O88]].
