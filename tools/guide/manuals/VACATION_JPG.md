# Vacation

A palm tree leaning over a rocky shore at sunset, with the sun on the sea
and a wooded headland on the right. It is a 640 by 480 photograph-style
picture stored as a JPEG, the ordinary kind a camera makes.

![Vacation open in PiXEL. A picture this size is read at half size and fitted to the window.](shot:pixel-window)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in all four sizes.

## Opening it

Double-click **VACATION.JPG** and PiXEL ([[PIXEL.O88]]) opens with the picture. A 640 by
480 picture is bigger than PiXEL's picture area, so PiXEL fits it to the
window; **1:1** on the toolbar shows it at its own size, and on a machine
short of memory PiXEL reads it at a half or a quarter of its size. Press
`F` for the whole screen in 256 colours on a VGA.

Press `Space` to step to the next picture in the folder, or `S` for a
slideshow of them all.
