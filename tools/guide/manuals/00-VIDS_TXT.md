# About these videos

`00-VIDS.TXT` is the list of demo videos on a video demo drive: what each
one shows, the kind of picture it uses, and what it needs to play its
best. Its words are shown below. Its name starts with `00` so it sorts to
the top of the folder, above the videos it describes.

## Where it is

In the `MEDIA` folder of each video demo drive, with a shortcut to it at
the top of the desktop. There are three versions:

- On the CGA drive it describes eleven videos for a CGA card: a picture
  made of coloured text, black and white, CGA's four colours, and the
  many colours a composite monitor shows.
- On the Hercules drive it describes seven black-and-white videos for a
  Hercules card.
- On the VGA drive it describes thirteen videos made for a 16 MHz 286 with
  a VGA card, two of them for a 66 MHz 486.

## Using it

Double-click it, or its shortcut on the desktop, to read it in Note Pad. Then double-click a video, or its shortcut on the desktop, to open
it in the Video Player and press `Space` or click **Play**. Most of the
videos want a Sound Blaster for their sound, and the file says which one
is made for the PC speaker and which need a composite monitor.
