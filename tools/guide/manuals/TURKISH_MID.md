# Turkish March

The Rondo alla Turca, the last movement of Mozart's Piano Sonata in A, K.
331 (1783). Both hands are played on a harpsichord: the tune in the right,
broken chords in the left, at 116 beats a minute. It plays for about 51
seconds.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. The Turkish March opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

The music is Mozart's and in the public domain. The arrangement was written
for os8088, and the arrangement and this file are released into the public
domain under CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **TURKISH.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it
from MIDIRack's playlist. It plays on an FM chip, a Sound Blaster or the PC
speaker, whichever the machine has.
