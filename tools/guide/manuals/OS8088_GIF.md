# os8088 logo

The os8088 logo, a black chip with the word **os8088** across it, on a grey
dotted ground. It is a 466 by 110 black-and-white GIF picture.

![The logo open in Paint.](shot:os8088-gif)

## Why it is on this disk

It sits in the `MEDIA` folder of the system disk, in every size, and of the
emulator system disk and the video demo drives. Paint's and PiXEL's
**File > Open...** start in `MEDIA`, so the first time you open a picture
there is one to find, even before a software disk goes in.

## Opening it

Double-click **OS8088.GIF** and Paint ([[PAINT.O88]]) opens with the logo. You can draw on
it, and save your version under a new name with **File > Save as Gif...**
or **Save as Bmp...**. PiXEL opens it too, with **File > Open...**.
