# Picnic Budget demo

The Picnic Budget is a small Weave spreadsheet app. It lists three picnic
items and their costs, and works out the total, the total rounded, the
average and whether the picnic comes in under 25. Change a cost and every
formula follows at once.

![The budget after changing Bread to 4.25: the total and the average follow.](shot:weave-sheet){full}

## What is inside it

One bundle file holding the grid's layout, its script and its worked-out
formulas. Its sources, [[SHEET.WML]], [[SHEET.WJS]] and [[SHEET.WFX]], ship
on the Loom disk.

## Using it

Double-click **SHEET.WAB** to run it in [[WEAVE.O88]]. Click a cell, type
into the formula bar and press `Enter`; **Show total** and **Cider +1** are
the app's own buttons. The [[WEAVE.O88]] manual covers the formulas it
understands.
