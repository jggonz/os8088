# Can-can

The Galop infernal from Jacques Offenbach's operetta Orpheus in the
Underworld (1858), the tune everyone knows as the Can-can. A trumpet takes
the melody with a piccolo above it, over an oom-pah piano and a snare drum,
at a brisk 150 beats a minute. It plays for about 39 seconds.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. The Can-can opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

The music is Offenbach's and in the public domain. The arrangement was
written for os8088, and the arrangement and this file are released into the
public domain under CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **CANCAN.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it from
MIDIRack's playlist. It plays on an FM chip, a Sound Blaster or the PC
speaker, whichever the machine has.
