# Bounce

A tiny animation: a red and white beach ball bouncing across the sand
under a blue sky and a yellow sun. It is a 96 by 72 GIF with ten frames
that loops for ever, and the smallest picture of the set.

![Bounce open in PiXEL, showing its first frame.](shot:bounce-gif)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. Unlike the other nine, it is drawn by a program in the os8088 source,
frame by frame. It is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in all four sizes.

## Playing it

A double-click on a GIF file starts Paint ([[PAINT.O88]]), which shows a picture and does
not play it. Open the ball in PiXEL instead: double-click another picture
in the folder and press `Space` until it appears, or start PiXEL and choose
**File > Open...**. Then press `A`, or choose **File > Play Animation**, to
set it bouncing. Press `A` again to stop.
