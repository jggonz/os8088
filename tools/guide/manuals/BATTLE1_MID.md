# Battle

**Steel Rain (Battle 1)**, original battle music in the style of a 1990s
computer game. Brass carries the tune over strings, a distorted guitar
chugging out the rhythm, a bass and drums, at a driving 150 beats a minute.
It is the longest of the ten songs, at about a minute and a half.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. Battle opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

It is an original composition, made for os8088 and quoting no existing
tune. The music and this file are released into the public domain under
CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 720KB, 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music
disk, MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live
USB image and CD. The 720KB software disk has room for only two songs, and
this is one of them.

## Playing it

Double-click **BATTLE1.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it, or pick it
from MIDIRack's playlist, where it comes first. It plays on an FM chip, a
Sound Blaster or the PC speaker, whichever the machine has.
