# RunCPM read me

`1STREAD.ME` is the read-me of RunCPM's CP/M master disk, written by
RunCPM's authors. It explains what is on drive A and what was changed from
Digital Research's originals: the patched programs, the original CP/M
sources included for reference, and the other command processors on the
disk and how to build them. Its words are shown below.

## Where it is

On the CP/M emulator disk in every size, and in the `RUNCPM` folder of the
everything set. There are two copies on each: one at the top of the disk,
beside `RUNCPM.O88`, where os8088 can open it, and one inside drive A's
user area 0, where CP/M programs see it. The two say the same, with small
differences between the versions.

## Using it

Open the copy at the top of the disk in Note Pad with **File > Open...**;
a double-click does not open a `.ME` file. From inside the CP/M emulator,
type `TYPE 1STREAD.ME` at the `A>` prompt.
