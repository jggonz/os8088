# Sheet markup

`SHEET.WML` lays out the Picnic Budget app: a heading, a grid of three
columns and eight rows, a status line that reports what you click, and two
buttons, **Show total** and **Cider +1**.

![The Picnic Budget app these files make, running in Weave.](shot:weave-sheet)

## Where it is

In the `LOOM` folder of the Weave disk and the Loom disk, in every size,
and of the second disk of the everything set. The compiled app it belongs
to is in the `WEAVE` folder beside it.

## Using it

Double-click **SHEET.WML** and Loom ([[LOOM.O88]]) opens the project, with
[[SHEET.WJS]] and [[SHEET.WFX]] beside it in the list. The packed result
of these sources is [[SHEET.WAB]].
