# WIN16

The `WIN16` folder holds the program that runs Windows 3.0 programs, and
the files that go with it:

- `WIN16.O88`, the program. Its fonts, dialogs and icons are inside it;
- `WIN16.TXT`, notes on the program;
- `COPYING.W16`, its licence.

There are no Windows programs in it. Bring your own and double-click them
in a Disk window: they open in WIN16 wherever they are.

## Where it is

In the `WIN16` folder of the 1.44MB and 1.2MB apps disks and of the second
720KB apps disk, on the everything set, and on the live USB image and CD.
On the 360KB emulators disk the three files are at the top of the disk,
beside RealDMG.

The disks that carry WIN16 also carry the `SYSTEM\APPDATA\WIN16` folder,
where Windows programs keep their settings. Keep the three files together
when you copy WIN16 to another disk.
