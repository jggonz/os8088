# After Hours

A ten-second original piece made for XTMP3, in mono. It is stored at 11,025
samples a second at 24 kbps, the kind of file an IBM PC or XT is meant to
play.

![XTMP3 with After Hours loaded on an IBM XT.](shot:xtmp3-window)

## Where it comes from

It was written for os8088 by a program that composes its notes, and encoded
to MP3. No recording or existing song is in it.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA` on the 1.44MB software disk and the music disk, on XTMP3's own
disk in every size, and on the live USB image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **AFTERHRS.MP3** and XTMP3 ([[XTMP3.O88]]) opens and plays it.
On a 4.77 MHz PC, XTMP3 decodes it to the hard disk first and then plays
the decoded file. Its ten seconds take a little under two minutes
to decode there.
