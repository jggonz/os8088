# House

A white wooden farmhouse with a porch, set among tall pines and maples
under a blue sky with clouds, with a lawn and a curving path in front. It
is a 320 by 240 PNG picture in full colour.

![House open in PiXEL.](shot:house-png)

## Where it comes from

It is one of ten sample pictures made for os8088 to show what PiXEL can
read. The scene was generated for the project and is not a photograph of a
real place; it is released under the same terms as the rest of os8088.

## Where it is

In the `PICTURES` folder of the PiXEL disk, in the 1.2MB and 1.44MB sizes. It is the largest of the ten files, and the smaller PiXEL disks leave it out; Balloons is the PNG they carry.

## Opening it

Double-click **HOUSE.PNG** and PiXEL ([[PIXEL.O88]]) opens with the picture at its own
size. In a window PiXEL shows a full-colour picture with the colours the
screen has, mixing dots of them for the shades in between; press `F` for
the whole screen, which shows many more colours on a VGA.
