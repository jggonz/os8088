# Logo video

`OS8088.V88` is a seven-second animation of the os8088 logo. A chip sits on
a circuit board with wires running out of it; ones and zeros travel along
the wires into the chip, the letters **os8088** burn themselves onto it one
by one, and a light sweeps across the package. The video then loops on the
moving bits with the logo in place, for as long as you leave it.

![The logo video playing in its window, on the desktop.](shot:video-window)

## What is inside it

One version of the video for each kind of screen: VGA, Hercules and CGA,
each drawn for that screen's own pixel shape so the chip looks the same on
all three. It is black and white and has no sound. The Video Player picks
the version that suits your screen.

## Why it is on these disks

It is the Video Player's sample, so there is a video to open on a machine
that has nothing else to play. It is in the `MEDIA` folder of the 720KB,
1.2MB and 1.44MB system disks and software disks, the 360KB music disk,
the video demo drives and the live USB image and CD. The 360KB system disk
has no room for it.

## Playing it

Double-click **OS8088.V88** and the Video Player ([[VIDEO.O88]]) opens with it. It is a
**Live** video: it plays in its window while the rest of the desktop keeps
working, so you can move the pointer, use the menus and drag the window
while it runs. Repeat is already on. The file is read into memory before
it starts, which takes a few seconds from a floppy, and it then plays with
no disk use at all.

`Space` pauses and resumes it, and `F` takes it to the whole screen and
back without stopping.
