# The Entertainer

Scott Joplin's ragtime piece from 1902. This arrangement plays the first
strain twice, a honky-tonk piano melody over a stride left hand (a bass note
on the beat and a chord off it), at 80 beats a minute, for about 52
seconds.

![MIDIRack playing Fur Elise, one of the other songs. The Entertainer opens the same way.](shot:furelise-mid)

## Where it comes from

The music is Joplin's and in the public domain. The arrangement was written
for os8088, and the arrangement and this file are released into the public
domain under CC0.

## Where it is

In `MEDIA/MIDI` on the 1.2MB and 1.44MB software disks, the music disk,
MIDIRack's own disk in every size, the video demo drives and the live USB
image and CD.

## Playing it

Double-click **ENTERTNR.MID** and MIDIRack ([[MIDIRACK.O88]]) opens and plays it. It is also
in MIDIRack's playlist whenever MIDIRack opens with nothing to play. It
plays on an FM chip, a Sound Blaster or the PC speaker, whichever the
machine has.
